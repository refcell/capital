Imposition of Penalty on KB Securities Co., Ltd. by The Korea Fair Trade Commission

On November 11, 2026, the Korea Fair Trade Commission (the “KFTC”) imposed an administrative fine of KRW 332,178,000,000 on KB Securities Co., Ltd. (“KB Securities”), a consolidated subsidiary of KB Financial Group Inc. The amount of the fine is equivalent to 3.87% of KB Securities’ equity capital of KRW 8,594,434,928,099 (which equals KB Securities’ equity capital on a consolidated basis as of December 31, 2025 plus the increases in share capital and capital surplus up to the date of this report). The penalty was imposed in connection with allegations of collusion among primary dealers participating in Korea Treasury Bond auctions.

The aforementioned date of imposition is based on the date on which KB Securities became aware of the matter through the KFTC’s press release, and the amount of the administrative fine is based on such press release which may be different from the final amount imposed by the KFTC. The official written decision from the KFTC and other related documents will be received at a later date, and KB Securities plans to carefully review such decision upon receipt and take relevant follow-up measures accordingly.
