Declaration of Quarterly Cash Dividends by KB Financial Group

On July 23, 2026, the board of directors of KB Financial Group Inc. (“KB Financial Group”) passed a resolution to declare a quarterly cash dividend of KRW 1,155 per common share for a total dividend amount of KRW 405,527,558,205, assuming the number of outstanding shares to be 351,106,111 shares.

The record date is August 7, 2026 (Korea local time), and in accordance with the Financial Investment Services and Capital Markets Act of Korea, the payment of such dividends is expected to be made within one month following the date of the resolution of the board of directors of KB Financial Group.

The total dividend amount is subject to adjustment based on the actual number of outstanding shares as of the record date, following the acquisition of treasury shares by KB Financial Group as disclosed in its report on Form 6-K titled “Resolution to Enter into a Trust Agreement for the Acquisition of Treasury Shares” dated July 23, 2026.
