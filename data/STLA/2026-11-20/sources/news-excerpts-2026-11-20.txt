== Stellantis Investor Day 2026 (May 21, 2026), FaSTLAne 2030 full presentation and transcript
https://www.stellantis.com/content/dam/stellantis-corporate/investors/events/stellantis-investor-day-2026/presentation/Stellantis-Investor-Day-2026-FaSTLAne2030-Full-Presentation.pdf
https://www.stellantis.com/content/dam/stellantis-corporate/investors/events/stellantis-investor-day-2026/transcript/Stellantis-Investor-Day-2026-FaSTLAne2030-Transcript-Full.pdf
"FASTLANE 2026-2030 FINANCIAL TARGETS: 2025 Actual / 2026 Guidance / 2028 Target / 2030 Target.
Net Revenues €154B / Mid-Single-Digit Increase / €175B / €190B.
Adjusted Operating Income Margin (0.5)% / Low-Single-Digit Margin / 5% / 7%.
Industrial Free Cash Flow €(4.5)B / Improved YoY; Positive in 2027 / €3B / €6B."
"2028 AOI MARGIN BRIDGE: 2025 Actual (0.5)%; Volume, mix, price (top-line growth drivers) 2.1%; Industrial cost savings 5.0% (VCP savings & higher plant capacity utilization); SG&A -0.6%; Cost headwinds -1.4% (fully driven by raw material inflation; lower regulatory cost offsets tariff increases in NA); Financial Services 0.5%; 2028 ~5%."
"Execute the Value Creation Program to deliver €6B annual run-rate of cost reductions by 2028."
"Deploy more than €60B investment in profitable growth over the plan period ... Hold average annual investment at 7% of Net revenues."
"Dividend policy maintained; target 25-30% dividend payout if liquidity >25% of Net revenues."
"INDUSTRIAL NET CASH BRIDGE: €6.7B (December 2025) ... €15B+ (December 2030); Liquidity ~€46B, 30% of net revenues ... ~€57B, 30% of net revenues; Hybrid Perpetual Notes issued in 2026."
2030 regional AOI margin targets: North America 8-10%, Enlarged Europe 3-5%, South America 8-10%, Middle East & Africa 10-12%, Asia Pacific 4-6%.
Transcript (CFO Joao Laranjo): "raw material cost increases that could represent up to the equivalent of 1% of revenue on a run rate basis in the following quarters of 2026."
