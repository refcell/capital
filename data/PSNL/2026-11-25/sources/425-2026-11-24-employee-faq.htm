Employee Equity FAQ

This FAQ summarizes the treatment of employee equity awards under the merger agreement and is intended for current Personalis employees. The transaction is expected to close on December 1, 2, or 3, 2026, subject to satisfaction of the remaining closing conditions. In the event anything herein conflicts with the merger agreement, applicable equity plan, or the applicable award documents, the merger agreement, applicable equity plan, and applicable award documents govern. Tax information only provides a general summary thereof and is not personal tax advice. Please consult your personal tax advisor regarding your equity awards, any prior option exercise and the receipt or sale of shares in connection with the transaction.

 

1.

What will happen to my Personalis stock, stock options and/or RSUs at the closing of the transaction?

At the closing, each outstanding share of Personalis stock will convert into the right to receive Tempus Class A common stock (the “stock consideration”). Tempus may elect to pay $16.25 in cash per share for a certain portion of the eligible shares, allocated proportionately among holders, subject to the merger agreement’s limits. Any fractional shares of Personalis stock will convert into cash consideration. Together, the applicable stock consideration and cash consideration are referred to as the “merger consideration” and are subject to required tax withholding and deductions. How your specific equity is treated depends on its type and, if applicable, vesting status:

Personalis Stock (including shares already issued upon RSU settlement, option exercise or ESPP purchase): Personalis shares will convert into the right to receive the merger consideration.

Vested In-the-Money Stock Options: Each vested and outstanding in-the-money stock option held by a current employee will be cancelled in exchange for the stock consideration in respect of the net number of shares of Personalis stock underlying such option (determined after a hypothetical net exercise of the option that gives effect to the exercise price).

Unvested In-the-Money Stock Options: Each unvested and outstanding in-the-money employee option will be assumed by Tempus and converted into an option to acquire Tempus Class A common stock. The number of Personalis shares underlying such option is multiplied by the exchange ratio and rounded down to the nearest whole share; the exercise price per share is divided by the exchange ratio and rounded up to the nearest whole cent. The converted option otherwise remains subject to the same terms and conditions, including vesting.

Out-of-the-Money Options: Each outstanding out-of-the-money stock option, whether vested or unvested, will be cancelled without any consideration being payable in respect thereof.

Outstanding Employee RSUs: Each outstanding employee RSU will be assumed and converted into a Tempus RSU, with the number of shares of Tempus Class A common stock subject to such award adjusted based on the exchange ratio. The converted RSUs otherwise remain subject to the same terms and conditions, including vesting.

Continued Employment: If you remain employed with Tempus following closing, the transaction itself does not interrupt your continuous service for purposes of converted equity awards. Your converted options will remain outstanding, and the post-termination exercise period will not be triggered. If your employment terminates for any reason upon or after closing, the termination provisions applicable to your original Personalis award will apply to your converted Tempus award.

 

2.

Will my Incentive Stock Options (ISOs) retain ISO tax status?

Unvested In-the-Money ISOs: An employee’s unvested in-the-money ISO is expected to retain its ISO status immediately following conversion into a Tempus stock option (to the extent they otherwise would have qualified as an ISO).

Vested In-the-Money ISOs: If you did not validly exercise your vested in-the-money ISOs by November 20, 2026, those options, to the extent still outstanding at closing, will be cancelled and settled as described in Question 1. The resulting stock consideration will be subject to applicable tax withholding upon receipt (and will no longer be considered an ISO).

Exercised ISOs: If you validly exercised your ISOs by November 20, 2026 and hold the resulting shares at closing, those shares receive merger consideration rather than the option treatment described in Question 1. Your tax treatment depends on the form of consideration and the applicable ISO rules and holding periods. Cash consideration received may result in a “disqualifying disposition” and may be taxed as ordinary income (although will not be subject to withholding); stock consideration may be eligible for capital gains treatment if specific ISO holding periods are met and there is a “qualifying disposition”. Exercising ISOs can trigger an Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT) (although if exercised in the same year as disposition, AMT will not apply and instead the spread at exercise will be taxed as ordinary income, although not subject to withholding), so please consult your personal tax advisor regarding your prior exercise and the receipt of merger consideration.

 

3.

What will happen to the ESPP?

Subject to completion of the merger, the Personalis 2019 Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP) will terminate immediately before the merger becomes effective. No offering period is currently in effect. See #1 above for the treatment of Personalis stock purchased pursuant to the ESPP.

 

4.

When my vested in-the-money stock options are converted to Tempus shares, how will withholding tax requirements be satisfied?

Payments in respect of vested in-the-money stock options are subject to applicable tax withholding and deductions. A “sell to cover” transaction will occur at the time the vested in-the-money stock options are converted to shares of Tempus Class A common stock to cover withholding taxes.

 

5.

When will our Personalis shares convert, and when will we receive the merger consideration?

Promptly after closing, the exchange agent will send transmittal materials and instructions to holders of record explaining how to surrender their shares (whether held in certificated or book-entry form) in exchange for the merger consideration. Once an employee properly surrenders their shares in accordance with those instructions, they will receive the applicable merger consideration. Employees holding shares through a brokerage account (book-entry) can generally expect this to happen automatically and relatively quickly after closing, while employees holding physical stock certificates will need to complete and return the transmittal materials and surrender their stock certificates before receiving their merger consideration.

6.

Will Tempus shares received for my Personalis shares and/or cancelled equity awards be held in my current E*TRADE account?

Yes. Once your Personalis shares and vested Personalis equity is converted to Tempus shares, it will be settled and deposited into your existing Personalis E*TRADE account.

 

7.

Where will my converted options and RSUs be administered?

Converted unvested stock options and RSUs will be deposited into a new account on a platform used by Tempus called Shareworks.

 

8.

Will my Personalis equity balance temporarily show as zero after the acquisition closes?

Yes. A temporary $0 balance is a normal part of the post-acquisition conversion process. After the deal closes, E*TRADE removes your legacy Personalis equity from view while they are being converted into Tempus awards. Your new balances will populate in your account once the conversion process is complete.

 

9.

What Is Shareworks?

Shareworks is an equity compensation administration platform. Tempus will provide Shareworks information for converted equity award holders, including login instructions, following closing.

 

10.

How soon after the close will we be able to access Shareworks?

We expect Shareworks to send emails within 10 days after the closing. As soon as practicable after the closing, you will receive an email from Shareworks to sign up for your account.

 

11.

When will the trading window be open?

The trading window will open the day after the merger closes and remain open through December 15, 2026, which is when Tempus enters its next restricted trading period. The trading window is then expected to reopen in late February or early March, following the release of Tempus’s annual and quarterly earnings.

 

12.

How is the exchange ratio calculated?

The exchange ratio is determined using the volume-weighted average price (VWAP) of Tempus Class A common stock on Nasdaq, rounded to the nearest four decimal places, over the 15 consecutive trading days prior to, but not including, the last trading day before closing. If the Parent Stock Price is greater than $48.42, the exchange ratio equals $16.25 divided by the Parent Stock Price. If the Parent Stock Price is $48.42 or less, the exchange ratio is fixed at 0.3356. The final ratio will be communicated once determined.

13.

Will Personalis employees be subject to Tempus’s insider trading policy and pre-clearance rules after closing?

Yes. The Tempus insider trading policy will be distributed to employees in the near future. Any employees subject to pre-clearance requirements will be notified in the coming weeks. All employees continue to have a personal responsibility to comply with company policies and laws regarding insider trading.
