Item 8.01 Other Events

As previously disclosed, on July 20, 2026, Personalis, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Personalis”), entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the “Merger Agreement”) by and among Personalis, Tempus AI, Inc., a Nevada corporation (“Tempus”), Aviary Development, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Tempus (“Merger Sub I”) and Toucan Development, LLC, a Nevada limited liability company and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Tempus (“Merger Sub II”). Pursuant to the Merger Agreement, and upon the terms and subject to the conditions therein, (a) Merger Sub I will be merged with and into Personalis (the “First Merger”), with Personalis surviving the First Merger as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Tempus (the “First Surviving Corporation”), and (b) as part of the same overall transaction, immediately after the First Merger, the First Surviving Corporation will merge with and into Merger Sub II, with Merger Sub II surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Tempus. Capitalized terms used in this Current Report on Form 8-K but not otherwise defined have the meaning set forth in the Merger Agreement.

Pursuant to Section 2.9 of the Merger Agreement, on November 24, 2026, Tempus notified Personalis of its determination to make a Parent Cash Election. Specifically, Tempus determined that the Cash Share Number is equal to 36,923,077 shares of common stock, $0.0001 par value per share, of Personalis (“Personalis Common Stock”) and, accordingly, that the aggregate amount of the Cash Consideration will be approximately $600 million assuming there are 108,740,631 shares of Personalis Common Stock issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time and that no demands for appraisal are perfected with respect to shares of Personalis Common Stock. The Cash Share Number is subject to automatic reduction (which, if applicable, would result in a corresponding increase in the share portion of the Merger Consideration) in accordance with Section 2.9 of the Merger Agreement to the extent necessary to satisfy the Reorg Threshold, determined using the Closing Date Parent Trading Price.

The Parent Cash Election is irrevocable. Because the Parent Cash Election has been made, at the Effective Time, each share of Personalis Common Stock issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time (other than the Cancelled Shares and the Dissenting Shares) (“Eligible Shares”) will be converted automatically into the right of the holder thereof to receive:

 

  •  

(i) an amount equal to $16.25 per share in cash, without interest (which may include a fraction of a share and, in that context, will be rounded to the nearest four decimal places), in respect of that number of Eligible Shares of such holder (which, for the avoidance of doubt, may include a fraction of an Eligible Share and, in that context, shall be rounded to the nearest four decimal places) equal to the product obtained by multiplying (x) the number of Eligible Shares held by such holder by (y) a fraction (the “Cash Consideration Fraction”), the numerator of which is the Cash Share Number and the denominator of which is the total number of shares of Company Common Stock issued and outstanding as of immediately prior to the Effective Time (other than Cancelled Shares) and (ii) a number of validly issued, fully paid and nonassessable shares of Tempus Class A Common Stock, equal to the Exchange Ratio for the holder’s remaining shares;

 

  •  

cash in lieu of any fractional shares of Tempus Class A Common Stock, calculated based on the Tempus Stock Price, without any separately bargained-for consideration; and

 

  •  

such amounts, if any, of post-closing distributions payable in respect of the Tempus Class A Common Stock issuable in the First Merger.

Absent any adjustment to the Cash Share Number under Section 2.9 of the Merger Agreement, the Cash Consideration Fraction is expected to be approximately 0.385815 being the Cash Share Number of 36,923,077 divided by 95,701,564, being the estimated number of shares of Personalis Common Stock issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time, excluding the estimated number of Cancelled Shares. The actual Cash Consideration Fraction and the Exchange Ratio will be calculated by the parties prior to closing in accordance with the Merger Agreement.

The closing of the transaction is subject to the adoption of the Merger Agreement by Personalis stockholders and other customary closing conditions.
