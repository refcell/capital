Core & Main Announces Preliminary Third-Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results, Updated Fiscal 2026 Outlook, Multiple Acquisitions
and Record Share Repurchases

▪Preliminary third quarter net sales of $2.12 billion to $2.13 billion, net income of $128 million to $140 million and Adjusted EBITDA (Non-GAAP) of $267 million to $275 million

▪Maintains fiscal 2026 net sales outlook and updates Adjusted EBITDA (Non-GAAP) outlook to $950 million to $960 million, from $950 million to $980 million

▪Completes three acquisitions with over $250 million in combined annual revenue

▪Repurchases a quarterly record $171 million of shares

ST. LOUIS, Nov. 10, 2026—Core & Main, Inc. (NYSE: CNM) (“Core & Main” or the “Company"), a leading specialty distributor dedicated to advancing reliable infrastructure with local service, nationwide, today announced, in advance of its participation at the 2026 Baird Global Industrial Conference, preliminary estimated financial results for the third quarter ended November 1, 2026, an updated fiscal 2026 outlook, its third consecutive quarter of record open-market share repurchases and the completion of three acquisitions. The Company expects to report full third quarter fiscal 2026 results on December 8, 2026.

Preliminary Third Quarter Results and Updated Fiscal 2026 Outlook

In the third quarter, Core & Main delivered 3% sales growth amid a mixed demand environment. Preliminary Adjusted EBITDA (Non-GAAP) was affected by higher transportation costs and investments in greenfields to drive long-term growth. Based on these preliminary results and current expectations for the remainder of the fiscal year, the Company maintains its fiscal 2026 net sales outlook of $7.8 billion to $7.9 billion and now expects fiscal 2026 Adjusted EBITDA (Non-GAAP) of $950 million to $960 million. The updated outlook continues to reflect stable municipal demand along with contributions from completed acquisitions, partially offset by softer-than-anticipated residential lot development and light commercial activity.

“We continue to execute our strategy across multiple avenues for growth, strengthening our platform through acquisitions in storm drainage, fusible HDPE and smart utility solutions and completing another record quarter of open market share repurchases,” said Mark Witkowski, Chief Executive Officer of Core & Main. “We also continue to invest organically in our business, opening a record eight greenfield locations year to date. We remain confident in our strategy and focused on the opportunities ahead as we continue to position Core & Main for long-term growth and value creation.”

Acquisitions and Share Repurchases

Core & Main completed the following acquisitions, representing over $250 million in combined annual revenue:

▪In September, Core & Main acquired Southwest Stormwater Solutions, which operates from one location in Phoenix, Arizona. Southwest Stormwater Solutions is a provider of proprietary underground stormwater vault systems for municipalities, contractors and civil engineers,

▪In October, Core & Main acquired Gajeske, LLC ("Gajeske"), based in Houston, Texas. Gajeske is a leading distributor of fusible HDPE pipe and fittings with deep technical expertise, providing fabrication, equipment rental and other value-added services from eight locations across Texas and Oklahoma; and

▪In November, Core & Main acquired KTI Limited and KTI Utility Services (collectively, "KTI"), based in Toronto, Canada. KTI is a distributor and utility solutions provider, offering comprehensive, turnkey meter and AMI solutions, including installation and related field services from seven locations across Ontario and Alberta.

In addition, Core & Main repurchased a record $171 million of shares in the open market during the third quarter of fiscal 2026, bringing year-to-date repurchases to approximately $428 million, with approximately $240 million remaining under its current share repurchase authorization.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

In addition to providing results that are determined in accordance with GAAP, the Company presents Adjusted EBITDA, which is a non-GAAP financial measure. This measure is not considered a measure of financial performance or liquidity under GAAP, and the items excluded therefrom are significant components in understanding and assessing the Company’s financial performance or liquidity. This measure should not be considered in isolation or as alternatives to GAAP measures such as net income, cash provided by or used in operating, investing or financing activities or other financial statement data presented in the Company’s financial statements as an indicator of the Company’s financial performance or liquidity.

The Company defines EBITDA as net income adjusted for depreciation and amortization, provision for income taxes and interest expense. The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as EBITDA as further adjusted for certain items management believes are not reflective of the underlying operations of the Company’s business, including but not limited to (a) loss on debt modification and extinguishment, (b) equity-based compensation, (c) expenses associated with the initial public offering and subsequent secondary offerings and (d) other income or other expense. Net income attributable to Core & Main, Inc. is the most directly comparable GAAP measure to Adjusted EBITDA.

The Company uses Adjusted EBITDA to assess the operating results and effectiveness and efficiency of its business. The Company presents this non-GAAP financial measure because it believes that investors consider it to be an important supplemental measure of performance, and it believes that this measure is frequently used by securities analysts, investors and other interested parties in the evaluation of companies in its industry. Non-GAAP financial measures as reported by the Company may not be comparable to similarly titled metrics reported by other companies and may not be calculated in the same manner. These measures have limitations as analytical tools, and you should not consider them in isolation or as substitutes for analysis of the Company’s results as reported under GAAP. For example, Adjusted EBITDA:

•does not reflect the significant interest expense or the cash requirements necessary to service interest or principal payments on debt;

•does not reflect income tax expenses, the cash requirements to pay taxes or related distributions;

•does not reflect cash requirements to replace in the future any assets being depreciated and amortized; and

•excludes certain transactions or expenses as allowed by the various agreements governing our indebtedness.

Adjusted EBITDA is not an alternative measure of financial performance or liquidity under GAAP and therefore should be considered in conjunction with net income and other performance measures such as gross profit or net cash provided by or used in operating, investing or financing activities and not as alternatives to such GAAP measures. In evaluating Adjusted EBITDA, you should be aware that, in the future, the Company may incur expenses similar to those eliminated in this presentation.

About Core & Main

Based in St. Louis, Core & Main is a leader in advancing reliable infrastructure® with local service, nationwide®. As a specialty distributor with a focus on water, wastewater, storm drainage and fire protection products and related services, Core & Main provides solutions to municipalities, private water companies and professional contractors across municipal, non-residential and residential end markets in the United States and Canada. With more than 370 locations, the Company provides its customers local expertise backed by a national supply chain. Core & Main’s 5,600 associates are committed to helping their communities thrive with safe and reliable infrastructure. Visit coreandmain.com to learn more.
