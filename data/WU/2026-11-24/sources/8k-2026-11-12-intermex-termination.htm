Item 1.02.

Termination of a Material Definitive Agreement.

As previously announced, on August 10, 2025, The Western Union Company, a Delaware corporation (the “Company” or “Western Union”), entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the “Merger Agreement”), by and among the Company, International Money Express, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“IMXI”), and Ivey Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of the Company (“Merger Sub”), providing for, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth therein, the merger of Merger Sub with and into IMXI (the “Merger”), with IMXI continuing as the surviving corporation in the Merger and becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company. Other material terms of the Merger Agreement were set forth in a Current Report on Form 8-K filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) on August 14, 2025.

Pursuant to the Merger Agreement, because the Merger was not consummated on or before November 10, 2026 (the “Outside Date”), each of the Company and IMXI had the right to terminate the Merger Agreement by providing written notice to the other party. On the evening of November 10, 2026, California’s Department of Financial Protection and Innovation (the “DFPI”) delivered unacceptable final Conditions of Approval that would have required material changes to the Company’s business model, agent relationships, and long-term business strategy, which Western Union was not required to accept under the terms of the Merger Agreement. Western Union viewed these conditions as substantial and not in the best interests of Western Union, its agents, or its shareholders.

Following receipt of the DFPI’s final Conditions of Approval, also on the evening of November 10, 2026, in accordance with the Merger Agreement, the Company delivered a notice of termination of the Merger Agreement to IMXI. Because termination becomes effective on the first business day following delivery of notice, the termination of the Merger Agreement became effective at 12:00 a.m. Eastern time on November 12, 2026. No termination fee is payable by either party to the other under the Merger Agreement in connection with the termination, and each party remains responsible for its own fees and expenses incurred in connection with the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereby.

The foregoing description of the Merger Agreement does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by the terms and conditions of the full text of the Merger Agreement, which was previously filed as Exhibit 2.1 to the Current Report on Form 8-K filed with the SEC on August 14, 2025, and is incorporated by reference herein.

Item 8.01.

Other Events.

On the evening of November 10, 2026, the Company received a response to its notice of termination from IMXI’s legal counsel, which asserted that the Company’s termination notice was invalid because the Company purportedly did not use reasonable best efforts to obtain approval from the DFPI under the Merger Agreement. The response letter also threatened legal action in the event that the Company did not consummate the Merger. On the morning of November 11, 2026, IMXI issued a press release reiterating this threat to pursue litigation. Western Union disagrees with the assertions in IMXI’s letter and the statements in IMXI’s press release and intends to vigorously defend any such lawsuit.
