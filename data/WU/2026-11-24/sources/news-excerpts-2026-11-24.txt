"International Money Express, Inc. (NASDAQ: IMXI) ("Intermex") today announced that it has filed suit against The Western Union Company (NYSE: WU) ("Western Union") in the Delaware Court of Chancery (the "Court") to enforce the August 10, 2025 Merger Agreement and compel Western Union to fulfill its contractual obligations to complete its acquisition of Intermex for $16.00 per share in cash."
"On November 10, 2026, Western Union delivered a notice purporting to terminate the Merger Agreement based on the agreement's Outside Date Termination Right. That same evening, the California Department of Financial Protection and Innovation notified Intermex and Western Union that it had issued a conditional approval of the merger."
"Intermex believes Western Union's purported termination is invalid given that all conditions to the closing have been satisfied. ... Intermex asserts that because Western Union was in breach of its obligations under the Merger Agreement, it had no right to terminate the transaction on the purported grounds. Intermex is seeking to enforce its contractual rights under the Merger Agreement, including through specific performance and other appropriate relief."
