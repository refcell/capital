## What We Expected

The May call rated ZETA Watch, "constructive if data and FCF quality hold". It valued the company on FY2030 free cash flow and EBITDA from a FY2026 revenue base of about $1.785 billion. The base case was 16% revenue growth a year to about $3.2 billion, a 14–15% free-cash-flow margin and 20–24x free cash flow, about $24 to $30 a share (midpoint $27). The bear was 10% growth and $14 to $17, the bull 22% and $40 to $50. It gave no weights, so its fair value was the $27 base. It said to watch organic growth excluding Marigold, super-scaled customers and ARPU, free-cash-flow conversion, stock compensation and buybacks. It set no kill criteria or probability claims.

## What Happened

Two releases have landed since the May call, both after the close: Q2 on August 4 and Q3 on November 9 (8-K Item 2.02).

Q3 revenue grew 46%, or 30% excluding Marigold's enterprise business ($48.0 million) and political candidate revenue ($7.4 million), up from 28% in Q2, and beat the midpoint of guidance by $22 million. It was the company's 21st consecutive "beat and raise" quarter. Super-scaled customer ARPU rose 22% to $2.0 million. Both the customer count and ARPU are "ahead of the 2028 model". GAAP net income turned positive in Q2 and reached $38.3 million in Q3. The FY2026 guide rose twice. Revenue went to $1,853–1,857 million from the $1,785 million midpoint before Q2, adjusted EBITDA to $414.1–415.2 million, and free cash flow to $270.6–271.6 million (14.6% of revenue). The Q4 revenue guide of $521–525 million implies 22–24% growth excluding M&A and political revenue, a slowdown from Q3's 30%.

Cash flow is real but carries a large stock-compensation cost. Nine-month operating cash flow was $210.3 million and company-defined free cash flow $181.2 million. Stock compensation was $155.1 million, 11.6% of revenue, and the company spent $85.5 million on buybacks. Shares outstanding rose from 244.8 million at the end of 2025 to 255.5 million on November 2. That includes about 4.55 million shares "issued in connection with certain agreements" in Q1 and Q2, and 326,686 more in Q3 under a strategic partnership that still obliges Zeta to issue $40 million of stock. The non-GAAP diluted count was 269.9 million in Q3, 5.3% more than a year earlier. In July Zeta replaced its credit facility with a $250 million term loan and a $750 million revolver, both due July 2031; the revolver is undrawn. It also made a $70.0 million loan advance, recorded in other non-current assets, without naming the borrower. After the quarter it bought Senso Agentic (about $27.5 million) and Social Audience (about $35.0 million), mostly for stock and earn-outs.

No Street estimate snapshot was captured for this update, so results are compared with the company's own guidance and the prior year.

## Estimate And Value Changes

The May values were FY2030 values with no horizon. This update states three-year values at the end of 2029, undiscounted. Each is FY2029 company-defined free cash flow at an exit multiple, plus net cash at the end of 2029, divided by the projected share count. Company free cash flow adds back stock compensation, so that cost is counted through dilution.

**Dilution.** The non-GAAP diluted count was 269.9 million in Q3 2026, 5.3% above the 256.3 million of Q3 2025. The nine-month count rose 4.1%, despite $85.5 million of buybacks. Only $78.5 million of the repurchase authorization was left at September 30, and it runs to the end of 2027. Some stock is already committed: a $40 million share obligation under a strategic partnership, and up to $26 million of stock earn-outs from the October deals. The base takes net dilution at 4% a year, the bear at 5% and the bull at 3.5%. Most awards are granted in dollars, so a lower share price means more shares for the same stock compensation.

**Net cash.** Net cash at the end of 2029 starts from $36.9 million today. It adds about $89.9 million of implied Q4 free cash flow and the cumulative 2027–2029 free cash flow. It subtracts $13.1 million of cash for the two October acquisitions, the remaining $78.5 million of buyback authorization, and $50 million a year of acquisitions and investments. That last figure is an assumption, near the $51.1 million spent in the first nine months of 2026. The $70 million loan advance is left out.

**Growth and margins.** Revenue starts from the $1,855 million guide midpoint. Political candidate revenue falls away in 2027, an off year, and Marigold is lapped. Growth then slows from 30% now. The base runs 20%, 16% and 12% (16% a year), the bear 10%, 7% and 4% (7%), and the bull 25%, 22% and 19% (22%). Free-cash-flow margins start from 14.6% in 2026. In the base they rise one point a year to 18%, against the May call's 14–15% for 2030.

**Exit multiples.** `python3 -m capital.multiples ZETA` puts ZETA at 35.7x trailing free cash flow, against its own 57-month median of 25.4x (p25 22.5x, p75 31.6x). The SIC 7372 peers in coverage have a median of 21.1x. The exits are 15x in the bear (where Box and CCC Intelligent Solutions trade, at 14.7x and 14.6x), 22x in the base (near the peer median and ZETA's own p25), and 30x in the bull (near ZETA's own p75).

The base case is worth 19% more than today's price, and the bull carries 43% of the fair value. At 30/50/20 the fair value would be $41.76, 6.0% a year. At 20/50/30 it would be $47.46, 10.6% a year. Watch holds across these weights. On base-case fundamentals, $35.07 implies an exit of about 18x FY2029 free cash flow. That is below the base case's 22x, so the market is not paying for more than the base case; it is paying for slightly less. The base growth sits at the 89th percentile of its size bucket. That is high for a base case, and the bucket measures five-year growth while these are three-year rates. The defense is that the base is a slowdown from 30% organic growth today and starts below the Q4 guide's 22–24%. The stance leans on the bull: with its weight moved to the bear (one third bear, two thirds base) the fair value would be $34.12, below the price. We keep 25% on the bull because it is roughly today's reported trend carried forward (organic growth in the 20s and margins expanding), not a new story. Zeta's initial guides have also come in below the result: 2026 started at a $1,785 million midpoint and is now $1,855 million, about 4% higher.

## Action

Raise the fair value to $44.61 from $27 and keep the stance at Watch. At $35.07 the value implies about 8.4% a year over three years, between the rule's 0% and 15% lines. The May call's tests passed and the business is worth more than it said. But the stock has risen 87%, today's 35.7x trailing free cash flow is above ZETA's own p75, and the share count grows about 4–5% a year. Kill criteria name what would move the stance: an initial 2027 revenue guide below 12% growth, FY2026 free cash flow below $260 million, or a non-GAAP diluted share count above 290 million in Q4 2027, about 6% a year, faster than even the bear's 5%. The next catalyst is FY2026 results and the first 2027 guide, expected around February 23, 2027 (last year's release was February 24, 2026, after the close).

## Independent Review

A second reader graded the first draft B, and a second round also B. It confirmed the figures and arithmetic and found four problems. The draft assumed 1.5–2.5% a year of net dilution, while the diluted count grew 5.3% in the past year despite buybacks. It assumed $120 million a year of buybacks with only $78.5 million of authorization left. It said the market was paying for today's growth, when the price implies about 18x base-case free cash flow, below the base's 22x. And its free-cash-flow kill criterion could barely fire. Its counter-case argued toward Avoid on owner cash flow after stock compensation. This version raises dilution to 4% a year in the base, caps buybacks at the remaining authorization, adds a growth glide path, rewrites the kill criteria and claims so they can fire, and states the variant view as a per-share dilution question. The second round confirmed the new values and asked that the upside triggers sit above the base case, that the dilution claim match the model, and that the note show how much the stance depends on the bull weight. Fair value is $44.61 and the stance is Watch.
