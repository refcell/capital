## What We Expected

The July call valued CNM on one-year forward EPS: a Yahoo FY27 EPS estimate of $2.81 (basis not stated; its Q1 figure matches GAAP rather than adjusted EPS) at 16.0x for a $44.96 base case, with a $32.40 bear (12.0x on $2.70) and a $63.00 bull (20.0x on $3.15), weighted 20/50/30 to $47.86. The thesis needed operating margin to recover from 9.3% as integration costs faded, with buybacks as the second engine. The named catalyst was the September quarter.

## What Happened

Two releases have landed since the July call.

**Q2 FY2026 (September 9, 8-K).** A clean quarter. Net sales rose 2.5% to $2,145 million, gross margin held at 26.7%, SG&A fell to 14.0% of sales from 14.4%, operating income rose 6.6% to $227 million, and adjusted diluted EPS rose 8.0% to $0.94. Management reaffirmed the FY2026 guide of $7,800 to $7,900 million of net sales and $950 to $980 million of adjusted EBITDA, and repurchased $169 million of stock.

**Q3 FY2026 preliminary (November 10, 8-K, after the close).** Ahead of a December 8 full report, the company pre-announced:

| Metric ($m) | Q3 FY2025 | Q3 FY2026 preliminary | Change at midpoint |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net sales | 2,062 | 2,120 to 2,130 | +3.1% |
| Net income | 143 | 128 to 140 | −6.3% |
| Adjusted EBITDA | 274 | 267 to 275 | −1.1% |

Management kept the FY2026 net sales outlook of $7.8 billion to $7.9 billion and trimmed adjusted EBITDA to $950 million to $960 million from $950 million to $980 million. The new midpoint implies an adjusted EBITDA margin of about 12.2%, the bottom of the prior 12.2% to 12.4% range. The stated causes are higher transportation costs, greenfield investment, and softer-than-anticipated residential lot development and light commercial activity; municipal demand is described as stable.

Capital deployment accelerated. Q3 repurchases were a record $171 million, bringing the year to about $428 million with about $240 million left on the authorization, and three acquisitions closed with over $250 million of combined annual revenue (Southwest Stormwater Solutions, Gajeske, and KTI).

No consensus snapshot was captured for this update, so results are compared with the company's own prior guide and the prior year, not with the Street.

## Kill Criteria And Pre-Mortem

The July call set no kill criteria, so this update sets them (see `call.json`): an FY2027 adjusted EBITDA guide below the FY2026 result, or net leverage above 3.0x adjusted EBITDA.

## Estimate And Value Changes

The July targets were one-year values on an EPS estimate of unstated basis. This update states three-year values (fiscal 2029) on the company's own adjusted diluted EPS, which adds back acquisition amortization ($0.37 in H1) and stock compensation, because the guide and the Q2 bridge are stated on that basis. GAAP EPS (H1 $1.34, tracking about $2.40 for the year) is the downside cross-check: every value below would be roughly a quarter lower on that basis.

**FY2026 adjusted EPS anchor.** H1 adjusted diluted EPS was $1.66. In Q2, $274 million of adjusted EBITDA produced $0.94 per share after about $40 million of depreciation and interest and a 22.3% tax rate on 193.4 million diluted shares. Q3 interest rose to $39 million, so the same conversion with about $47 million of depreciation and interest, applied to Q3 ($267–275 million) and to the Q4 the guide implies, gives $3.10–3.15 for the year. The model uses $3.10, the low end. The bear anchor of $2.95 assumes Q4 adjusted EBITDA of $150 million, 19% below the $184 million the guide implies.

## Action

Lower near-term earnings expectations, keep the stance at Watch. At $47.44 the probability-weighted value implies about 5.0% a year over three years, inside the rule's Watch band (0% to 15%). Per-share growth now leans more on buybacks and acquisitions than on margin, and part of the buyback is debt-funded while interest expense is rising, which is a lower-quality mix. The next catalyst is the full Q3 report on December 8, 2026, then FY2026 results and the first FY2027 guide in March 2027.

## Independent Review

A second reader reviewed two drafts. The first graded D: its $2.75 EPS anchor matched neither the adjusted (about $3.1) nor the GAAP (about $2.4) basis in the filings. The second graded B and caught that Q3 interest expense had risen to $39 million, which the draft had missed. This version re-derives the anchor with that interest, cuts base growth to 5% a year to match its own drivers, shifts weight to the bear case, and derives the bear anchor. The reviewer's counter-case is recorded in `call.json`: if FY2027 EBITDA is flat, higher interest offsets the buyback and the stock is worth about $40–43 at 13–14x. That outcome sits close to the bear case, which now carries 30% weight.
