## What We Expected

The July call was backfilled from a report written from Yahoo Finance data. Several of its premises were wrong:
- It treated NRC certification as pending ("expected in 2026-Q3 or 2027-Q1"). The 10-Q says the NRC approved NuScale's 77 MWe, six-module design in May 2025, after approving the 50 MWe design in September 2020.
- Its $890 million of cash counted only cash and short-term investments at March 31.
- Its 346 million shares predated most of the 2026 ATM sales.
- It described the 2023 cancellation as "Boise". The project was the Carbon Free Power Project of UAMPS (the 10-Q's "CFPP LLC").

It valued NuScale as a cash floor plus an option. It gave values of $0.60 / $3.00 / $16.00 at 30/50/20 and rated the stock Watch. The recorded fair value of $2.89 is probably a backfill mis-extraction: it equals the report's $2.89 billion market cap, the scenario weights give $4.88, and the report's conclusion was $5.70–7.80. This update compares against both $2.89 and $4.88. Its monitoring list asked for cash above $800 million, burn below $150 million a quarter, revenue above $5 million a quarter, a firm customer order, and no raise below $5 a share. It set no kill criteria or claims.

NuScale sold 89.7 million Class A shares under its 2026 ATM for $1.0 billion gross ($984.5 million net), an average of $11.14. The program finished in June, before the July call. A new $750 million ATM was signed on August 11 and had not been used by October 30: the Class A count was 410,541,392 at September 30 and 410,568,229 on October 30. Nine-month operating cash outflow was $401.4 million. $259.9 million of that settled the rest of the ENTRA1 Milestone Contribution 1, so underlying use was $141.5 million, after $43.9 million of investment income. That is about $60 million a quarter before interest.

Commercially, nothing became binding:
- **TVA.** ENTRA1 and TVA signed a non-binding agreement in September 2025 to develop up to 6 GW. NuScale says it is "positioning ourselves to support the potential next phase", which "is contingent on the execution of one or more power purchase agreements between ENTRA1 and TVA".
- **RoPower.** On February 12, 2026, Romania's government approved the investment decision for the Doicești plant. That approval allows RoPower to seek financing for feasibility and site-specific design work before any construction.
- **Production.** In October, NuScale contracted Doosan Enerbility to fabricate the upper reactor coolant system of its first module, its first move from design to physical production. Long-lead material on the balance sheet is $69.7 million.

The ENTRA1 Partnership Milestones Agreement is the most important term the July call missed. ENTRA1 is NuScale's "exclusive global partner" for commercialization. NuScale pays ENTRA1 a Milestone Contribution for each module placed in an ENTRA1 project. 15% is due on a non-binding agreement, 35% on a binding PPA or offtake agreement, and 50% on an OEM agreement for the modules. The first tranche for 72 modules cost $507.4 million, which implies about $46.98 million a module in total. With 15% already paid on those 72, about $39.9 million a module remains: $16.4 million when a binding PPA is signed and $23.5 million when an OEM agreement is. Binding PPAs for all 72 modules would trigger about $1.18 billion more, and OEM agreements for them about $1.69 billion. Revenue from module sales comes later, at a negotiated maximum price that is not disclosed. No liability for the later tranches is accrued.

The agreement is lopsided. ENTRA1 "retains sole and full discretion to select, contract with, or purchase from NuScale", while NuScale "must obtain ENTRA1's consent to pursue opportunities without their involvement". The PMA runs to 2045, renews automatically, and either side can end it early mainly on the other's insolvency.

ENTRA1's credentials are now in court. In July 2026 the Truedson securities class action, which already named NuScale and Fluor, was amended to add ENTRA1 and four individuals as defendants. It alleges false and misleading statements about "ENTRA1's experience, qualifications and capabilities as a developer of nuclear power plants" (class period March 3 to November 6, 2025). NuScale moved to dismiss on September 25. The opposition is due November 16 and NuScale's reply December 16, 2026. Two derivative suits rest on the same allegations: Leigh (March 2026, stayed) and Harwood (October 28, 2026). No loss is estimated or accrued.

## Kill Criteria And Pre-Mortem

The July call set no kill criteria, pre-mortem or claims, so this update sets them (see `call.json`). Each sits on the bear path, where no binding order arrives by the end of 2029; the base and bull both have a binding PPA during 2028.

- **TVA path ends.** TVA publicly ends its collaboration with ENTRA1, cuts the 6 GW scope, names another vendor's technology for any site in the program, the PMA is terminated, or ENTRA1 enters insolvency proceedings. Check by December 31, 2027.
- **No binding offtake.** No binding PPA or offtake agreement for NuScale modules is signed by ENTRA1 or an affiliate by June 30, 2029. That leaves six months of slack after the base's 2028 PPA, so a one-quarter slip does not fire it.

A fired criterion rules out the base and bull as defined, so the reweighting is not 55/35/10. We would then value the call mostly on the bear. Even giving 15% to a binding offtake arriving in the second half of 2029 or later, valued at cash plus $1.5 billion of EV ($6.14 a share), the value is about $3.29, below the price and so Avoid, unless the stock has already fallen below about $3.30.

## Estimate And Value Changes

The July values had no horizon. This update states values at the end of 2029, undiscounted. Each value is an assumed end-2029 enterprise value plus end-2029 cash, divided by the shares outstanding then. Cash is rolled forward from $1,864.1 million:
- less about $55 million of net use in Q4 2026;
- less each scenario's annual cash cost (operating spending, capex and production investment);
- less Milestone Contributions as they fall due;
- plus 3.5% interest on the balance;
- plus any equity raise, net of a 2% fee.

The share count starts at 434.7 million (Class A, Class B and RSUs) and grows by 4 million a year from equity awards. The 4.47 million options are excluded.

- **Bear.** ENTRA1 never converts the TVA framework, RoPower does not reach construction, and NuScale keeps spending about $240 million a year to stay ready. Because ENTRA1 holds the exclusive commercialization rights until 2045 and decides whether to buy, NuScale cannot easily go around it. The market values the approved design and the option at about what they cost to keep alive, so the stock trades at cash. The bear has no binding offtake by the end of 2029. Its EV of $0 is deliberately conservative: the market would probably still price some option on a later order, worth about $0.50 a share on the post-kill branch above. It may also be generous: a company spending $240 million a year with no order can trade below cash, which the sensitivity below covers ($1.71). The bear could also be too high if the company spends its cash on a project that fails.
- **Base.** In 2028 ENTRA1 signs a binding PPA with TVA for a first 12-module plant. By 2029 an OEM agreement covers the first six modules. NuScale pays $338 million of Milestone Contributions, spends $300 million a year on production readiness, and raises $500 million at $7 on the news. The $3 billion end-2029 EV is a judgment, not a valuation: no module has been sold, so no earnings or peer multiple anchors it. For scale, it is about a quarter of the more than $11 billion the market put on NuScale in October 2025 on the non-binding TVA framework (month-end $44.87 on at least 278 million shares). It is also about $3.2 billion per GW of the first 0.92 GW plant, for a design that would then have a binding order and a 72-module pipeline. Module revenue would still be in 2030 or later. The sensitivities below matter more than the point estimate.
- **Bull.** TVA signs PPAs for 36 modules and OEM agreements for 24, RoPower proceeds, and NuScale raises $1.5 billion at $15 to pay $1.16 billion of Milestone Contributions. The $10 billion EV is still below the October 2025 level. Customer deposits on module orders, which the model ignores, would add to cash.

**What the price implies.** With the bull set aside, $5.22 sits 46% of the way from the bear ($2.79) to the base ($8.13). The market is pricing a bit under even odds of a first binding order by 2029. We put 45% on an order (base plus bull), close to the market, and our 0.45 claim on a binding offtake by the end of 2028 matches that.

**Sensitivities:**
- Each $1 billion of base EV moves the base by $1.93 and the fair value by $0.67. At a $2 billion base EV the fair value is $5.76, 3.3% a year; at $4 billion it is $7.10, 10.8% a year.
- Valuing the bear at cash less two years of spending ($480 million) cuts the bear to $1.71 and the fair value to $5.84, 3.8% a year.
- Spending $40 million a year more in the bear and base cuts the fair value to $6.19, 5.9% a year.
- Combining the weaker bear and a $2 billion base EV at 60/30/10 gives about $4.95, below the price.

## Action

Keep the stance at Watch with a neutral bias, and set the fair value at $6.43, against the July call's stated $2.89 (weighted $4.88).

The July values sat near the cash floor because the report assumed an NRC approval that had already happened and a cash pile half its real size. With $4.29 a share of cash and no debt, the downside to the bear ($2.79 at the end of 2029) is spending, not insolvency. The upside needs ENTRA1 to sign a binding order, and NuScale pays about $40 million a module as that happens.

The weighted return of 7.2% a year is in the middle of the Watch band. It moves into Own at 40/40/20 or at a price near $4.23, and into Avoid if a kill criterion fires. This call has no strong variant view. The analytical point the July call missed, and the headline cash figure hides, is that a binding order costs NuScale cash before it earns any.

Moves toward Own:
- a binding PPA between ENTRA1 and TVA for NuScale modules;
- a price at or below about $4.23.

Moves toward Avoid: either kill criterion, namely
- TVA ends or narrows the ENTRA1 path, the PMA ends, or ENTRA1 becomes insolvent;
- no binding offtake by June 30, 2029.

Also watch the Truedson motion to dismiss. A ruling that lets the claims about ENTRA1's capability proceed would move weight to the bear.

The next catalyst is Q4 2026 results, expected around February 25, 2027. Last year's came after the close on February 26, 2026.

## Independent Review

An independent reviewer (agent:reviewing-research) read two drafts of this note against the sources and reproduced every value.

- **Round 1, grade B−.** It found these problems:
  - 50% weight on a 2028 PPA contradicted a 0.40 claim;
  - the kill criteria sat on the base case's own deadlines, and the post-kill reweighting kept weight on outcomes it ruled out;
  - the headline put the PMA cost at $47 million a module rather than about $40 million still owed;
  - the Truedson suit naming ENTRA1, the derivative suits and ENTRA1's discretion and consent terms were missing;
  - the July checklist was reported selectively;
  - the July $2.89 baseline was probably a mis-extraction;
  - the EVs had no cross-check.
- **Round 2, grade B.** Most fixes held. Problems still open:
  - the bear valued the no-order world at EV $0 while the post-kill branch valued it at $3.29;
  - the note said Fluor was added to Truedson in July, when it was an original defendant;
  - "narrows" was vague and the offtake criterion fires late;
  - three figures were rounded wrongly.

**Changes after round 2:**
- The bear is defined as no binding offtake by end-2029, with EV $0 called deliberately conservative.
- The post-kill branch is relabelled.
- The Fluor wording is corrected.
- The first kill criterion now names the 6 GW scope and other vendors.
- The rounding is fixed.

We did not adopt one point. We kept the offtake kill criterion at June 30, 2029 rather than adding an earlier spending test: the bear's spending ($240 million a year) does not differ from the base enough to separate them before an order. The early test that remains is the TVA and ENTRA1 criterion, checked by the end of 2027.
