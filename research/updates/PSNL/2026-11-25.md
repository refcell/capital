## What We Expected

The August 25 call valued Personalis as a standalone cancer-genomics company on price-to-sales and a cash floor: a $6.38 bear, $18.96 base and $59.80 bull, with a stated probability-weighted fair value of about $26. It named gross margin as the variable to watch and the Q3 report as "the next inflection test". It made no dated claims and set no kill criteria.

That call missed the most important fact about the stock. On July 20, 2026, five weeks before it was written, Personalis signed an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Tempus AI. The "$16.25 target" the August report attributed to a Lake Street downgrade is the deal's per-share cash price. From July 20 on, the most a holder could receive was the merger consideration, so the $26 fair value and the $59.80 bull case were never reachable while the deal stood. The August stance of Watch happened to be harmless, but its numbers described a stock that no longer existed.

Personalis no longer gives guidance or holds earnings calls because of the merger.

**The merger.** Each eligible Personalis share converts at closing into Tempus Class A stock at an Exchange Ratio of $16.25 divided by the Parent Stock Price, which is TEM's 15-trading-day VWAP ending the day before the last trading day before closing. Below a $48.42 Floor Price the ratio is fixed at 0.3356, and Personalis may walk away if the VWAP is below $46.00. Tempus may pay up to half the shares in cash at $16.25. On November 24 it made that election for 36,923,077 shares, about $600 million (8-K filed November 25): with 95,701,564 eligible shares (Tempus's own stake is cancelled), each holder gets cash for 38.58% of their shares and stock for the other 61.42%.

The remaining steps are close to done. The HSR waiting period expired on October 2, the UK CMA had no further questions, and the S-4 was declared effective (October 20 in the 10-Q, October 21 in the November 8-Ks). ISS and Glass Lewis recommend voting for the deal (8-K, November 23). Merck (about 13%) has signed a voting agreement and Tempus holds about 12%; 27.1 million other shares must vote yes, because approval needs a majority of all outstanding shares. The special meeting is November 30, and an employee FAQ filed under Rule 425 expects closing on December 1, 2 or 3.

**What a share is worth now.** Per PSNL share, the package is $6.27 of cash (0.3858 × $16.25) plus 0.6142 × $16.25 / VWAP Tempus shares. TEM's slide means the VWAP, still carrying early-November prints of $75 to $84, sits well above today's price, so the stock half is worth less than its nominal $16.25. On daily bars (typical price weighted by volume) the VWAP comes out near $64.81 for a December 2 close, with the two missing days filled at $54.83. That gives an Exchange Ratio of about 0.2507, or 0.154 TEM shares per PSNL share.

Each day of delay drops an old $75 to $84 print from the window, so a later close means more TEM shares. At an unchanged TEM the package is worth 0% to 3% more than PSNL's $14.53. That gap is smaller than TEM's typical daily move, and the typical-price proxy can be off from the true VWAP by more than the gap.

The two safety features do not bind. The Reorg Threshold, which would reduce the cash portion if stock fell below 40% of value, only applies if TEM falls below about $27. The walk-away right needs a VWAP below $46, against about $63 to $66.

## Kill Criteria And Pre-Mortem

The August call set no kill criteria. This update sets two: the merger agreement is not adopted at the November 30 meeting or its adjournment (check by December 4), and the merger is not closed by the April 20, 2027 Outside Date.

The pre-mortem now concerns the closing week. Turnout could fall short, since every unvoted share counts against the deal; the warning would be a postponement or use of the adjournment proposal. TEM could keep falling into the close, which cuts the stock half directly: at $43.86, 20% below November 24, the package is worth $13.19. A closing condition such as the tax opinion or the Nasdaq listing of the new TEM shares could fail and push the close out by weeks, which leaves holders carrying TEM and break risk for longer; a slip of a few days is not the risk, because each day drops a high print from the VWAP and raises the ratio (at a flat TEM, $15.26 for a December 4 close). And the VWAP proxy could be wrong; a final Exchange Ratio below 0.245 would show that.

## Claims Resolved

The August call made no dated probability claims, so nothing resolves. This update makes four, all due by mid-December: approval at the November 30 meeting (97%), closing by December 11 (93%), a final Exchange Ratio between 0.240 and 0.270 (75%), and a Cash Consideration Fraction between 0.380 and 0.390 with no Reorg Threshold cut (95%). The ratio claim assumes a close by about December 4; a later close at a flat TEM, or a December 3 close with TEM 20% lower, pushes the ratio above 0.270.

## Estimate And Value Changes

Scenario values are now values per PSNL share at closing on December 2, not three-year values. A deal break is the only path back to standalone value. For that case the August thesis's own logic, the July 16 unaffected price of $14.68 and the proxy's standalone ranges (Centerview: $8.95–14.15 on trading comparables, $11.05–16.75 on DCF) set the range. The break case is valued at $12.00, below the $14.68 unaffected price but inside both Centerview ranges: TEM is roughly where it was on July 16, so the sector has not de-rated, but a failed vote would leave a company that has burned about $22 million of cash and investments since June 30 ($212.7 million to $190.6 million), loses $35 million a quarter at the operating line and must restart its ATM to fund the plan.

The ±20% band is about TEM's own move over the previous two weeks. The weights reproduce a 98% chance of closing with TEM equally likely up or down 20%, plus a 2% break: 0.98 × $14.70 + 0.02 × $12.00 = $14.64. The 2% break odds are a judgement: every regulatory step is done, the proxy advisers support the deal and a quarter of the votes are committed. The price is consistent with that: with a $14.71 package and a $12.00 break value, $14.53 implies about 7% break odds for a December 2 close, but close to none for a December 1 close ($14.54), and the VWAP proxy alone can move the package by $0.10–0.20.

## Action

Lower fair value to $14.64 from $26 and keep Watch. The expected return to the close is about +0.7%, inside the error of the VWAP estimate. Anyone who could short TEM against PSNL could lock most of that gap, but it is about $0.10–0.20 a share for a week of deal risk, so there is no unhedged trade in PSNL. Anyone holding PSNL is effectively long 0.154 TEM shares plus $6.27 of cash per share, and whether to keep that TEM exposure is a TEM question this repo has not researched. The next catalysts are the November 30 vote and the closing 8-K. The next review is December 4, 2026: it resolves the vote, closing, Exchange Ratio and cash-fraction claims and drops PSNL from coverage once the stock is delisted.

The lesson for the repo: the August report quoted the $16.25 deal price as an analyst target and did not see the merger. The data store could not have flagged it, because `data/calendar/earnings.csv` keeps only Item 2.02 and periodic filings; the July 20 8-K appears there as items 2.02, 7.01 and 9.01, and Item 1.01, Rule 425 filings and the DEFM14A are not captured. A check of the EDGAR submissions list for Item 1.01, 425, DEFM14A or SC 13E3 filings before valuing a name would have caught it.
