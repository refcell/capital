By segment, Q3 adjusted EBITDA rose $507 million in the East, mostly from the LS Power portfolio and CPower acquired in January plus higher capacity prices, and $211 million in Texas on retail margins and favorable weather. Texas was flat for nine months ($1,615 million against $1,618 million) because a mild winter offset the summer. Vivint Smart Home rose $39 million on customer adds.

Guidance: 2026 was reaffirmed at $7.90–9.90 of adjusted EPS and $5,325–5,825 million of adjusted EBITDA. Nine-month adjusted EPS of $7.68 leaves $0.22–2.22 for Q4. The first 2027 guide is $8.05–10.05 of adjusted EPS (midpoint $9.05, on 207 million basic shares) and $5,500–6,000 million of adjusted EBITDA, with $1,300 million of interest, $2,115 million of depreciation, and an adjusted tax rate of about 17%. It excludes the asset sale. The $9.05 midpoint is 18.9% below the $11.16 FY2027 Yahoo consensus the August call used, and only 1.7% above the 2026 midpoint, because higher depreciation and interest absorb the EBITDA growth and the per-share gain comes from the lower share count.

The asset sale: NRG agreed to sell six gas-fired plants totaling 4.9 GW in Texas and the East to LS Power for $4 billion in cash, targeted to close in Q2 2027 subject to HSR, FERC, and New York PSC approval. It keeps the whole PJM portfolio and CPower. The 10-Q expects a $400–600 million impairment, mostly goodwill, when the plants are classified as held for sale in Q4. The proceeds fund an incremental $1 billion buyback that starts before the end of 2026 (the board added $1 billion of authorization on November 6) and $2 billion of debt reduction "to maintain the Company's current leverage profile". The last $1 billion is left for contracted growth or more debt reduction. The base 2027 plan already had $1 billion of debt reduction, so planned 2027 debt reduction totals $3 billion. Updated 2027 guidance comes after closing. Neither filing gives the EBITDA of the plants being sold.

The balance sheet carries the January acquisition: long-term debt was $23,048 million at September 30 against $16,565 million at the end of 2025, including $1,016 million drawn on the revolving credit facility, and total liquidity fell to $6.0 billion from $9.6 billion. By October 31 the revolver draw was down to $400 million and $620 million was drawn on the receivables facility, so the two lines together were about flat. The 2026 buybacks of $963 million through September were made at an average of $154.45 a share, against $97.11 now; only $14 million more was repurchased in October, at $99.62. The 1.2 GW BYOP data center project "remains on track"; no customer contract was announced.

No Street estimate snapshot was captured for this update, so results are compared with the company's own guide, the prior year, and the consensus figures recorded in the August call.

## Estimate And Value Changes

The August values were five-year terminal values built on Yahoo consensus EPS. This update states three-year values (FY2029) on NRG's company-adjusted EPS, which excludes fair-value changes on derivatives, because the guide is stated on that basis. GAAP EPS swings with those hedges (Q3 GAAP $2.59 against adjusted $4.68), so it is not a usable anchor here. The two fair values are not directly comparable.

**Asset sale estimate.** The filings give no EBITDA for the six plants, so the model infers it. Keeping leverage constant while repaying $2 billion implies about $500 million of EBITDA leaves ($2,000 million divided by 4.0x, the ratio of $23,048 million of debt to the $5,750 million 2027 EBITDA midpoint), or 8x for the plants. Depreciation of about $150 million leaves with them. Neither filing gives the plants' book value or useful life, so this uses the January acquisition as a proxy: the 10-Q's purchase price allocation puts $9,717 million of plant on about 13 GW, or about $750 per kW, which is about $3.66 billion for 4.9 GW; over an assumed 25-year remaining life that is about $146 million a year. The $4 billion price plus the $400–600 million impairment, a carrying value of about $4.5 billion including goodwill, is consistent with it. Interest saved on $2 billion at 5.6% (the guide's $1,300 million on $23,048 million) is about $113 million. Pre-tax earnings fall about $237 million, or about $197 million after the guide's adjusted tax rate of about 17%. That takes 2027 adjusted net income from the $1,870 million midpoint to about $1,673 million. The incremental $1 billion buyback at about $100 retires about 10 million shares, to about 197 million, so pro forma run-rate EPS is about $8.50, 6% below the $9.05 midpoint. If the last $1 billion of proceeds also goes to buybacks, the dilution falls to about 1% ($1,673 million on 187 million shares is $8.95). In value terms the sale is slightly accretive: at NRG's own 7.6x the plants would be worth about $3.8 billion against $4.0 billion received, or about $1 a share.

The values are driven by per-share earnings, not revenue; the revenue rates only place each case against base rates, and the sale removes an unknown share of reported revenue.

The exit multiples are anchored on the stock's own pricing, since no peer or historical multiples were captured. 11x is the 11.4x the stock trades at on pro forma 2027 EPS. 14x is about the 13.4x the August call recorded on FY2026 consensus, before that consensus proved 19% too high for 2027. 8x is the multiple for a levered generator whose earnings stall; the stock closed at 9.5x pro forma EPS ($80.55) four days before the release. Base EPS of $9.55 at 14x would be $133.70, about 11% a year; that re-rating is the bull case, not the base. At 10x instead of 11x the base would be $95.50 and the fair value about $99.86, or about 1% a year. The nearer rating boundary is Avoid, not Own: Own needs about $148. The rule rating is price-only; the $1.90 dividend (about 2% a year, rising to $2.05) would add to the return but is not counted.

## Action

Lower the value and keep the stance at Watch. At $97.11 the probability-weighted value implies about 2.5% a year over three years, inside the rule's Watch band (0% to 15%). The quarter itself was strong and operating cash flow answered the August call's main worry, but the 2027 guide shows that per-share growth now comes from buybacks rather than earnings growth, the incremental buyback is set to start before the sale proceeds arrive, which risks bridging it with credit lines, and the sale cuts earnings to hold leverage flat. The next catalyst is FY2026 results and any update on the sale's approvals around February 23, 2027, then the sale closing and updated 2027 guidance in mid-2027.

The note now states that sensitivity; the stance stays Watch because the guide was reaffirmed and the 10x outcome still clears 0%.
