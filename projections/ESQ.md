## Extrapolation Notes

- FY27-$292.8M revenue consensus from Yahoo Finance Analysis is flagged as extrapolated: there is only 1 analyst covering, and the implied +51.3% YoY growth from $193.5M in FY26 to $292.8M in FY27 seems aggressive for a $2.4B-asset bank. The base projection uses $207M for FY28 (7% growth from $193M in FY27) which is below the consensus of $292.8M for FY27. This is intentional: the consensus appears inflated for a single-analyst estimate in a niche bank.
- FY2027+ EPS growth is extrapolated from ROE and BVPS compounding assumptions, not from the analyst consensus of $8.52. At 15% ROE (base) and 50% retention (dividend payout is only 12%), BVPS compounds at 7% and EPS compounds at approximately 7%, not 25.7% as the consensus implies. The FY27 consensus of $8.52 is treated as an outlier that the base case does not chase.
- The projections do not incorporate the Rule 425 filing (June 23, 2026) or any of the 8-K filings (May-Jul 2026) beyond flagging them as potential catalysts in the research report. If those resolve into a corporate transaction, the entire projection framework requires recalibration.
- Net income margin is held at 33.0% across all cases and all horizons. This is consistent with the FY2022-FY2025 track record (33.8-36.1% range). If the rate cycle turns and NIM compresses, the margin could drop to 25-30%, which would require a revision of all projections downward.
- FCF-related metrics are marked N/A throughout because banks have no meaningful free cash flow — deposits offset loan origination and the OCF-Capex lens is not applicable.
