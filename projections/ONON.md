## Current Run-Rate

On generated CHF3.220 billion of TTM revenue, CHF443.5 million of operating income, CHF396.2 million of net income, and CHF437.9 million of FCF through June 2026. Gross margin was 64.82%, operating margin 13.77%, and FCF margin 13.60%. Cash and investments of CHF1.239 billion exceeded CHF562.5 million of debt. The projection therefore begins from a profitable, net-cash business rather than assuming a turnaround.

The uncertainty is growth quality after the Q2 miss. Constant-currency sales still grew 21.6%, DTC grew approximately 34%, and gross margin reached 65.4%, but reported revenue missed expectations and management moderated its FY2026 outlook. Bear, base, and bull cases primarily vary revenue durability, normalized margins, and the terminal multiple. Interim stock prices are mechanical paths to explicit year-10 values, not one-year targets.

## Scenario Read

The one-year revenue range brackets visible consensus. Bear revenue is below CHF3.51 billion translated to USD, base matches it, and bull is modestly above. The FY2027 base growth rate is close to the visible 18.62% headline. Later years are scenario estimates, not sell-side consensus.

The bear case is the most important discipline. On can remain a viable, growing brand and still deliver a slightly negative decade-long stock return if normalized margins weaken and dilution continues. The base supports a roughly 10% annualized price return, while the bull requires On to become a much larger multi-category franchise. No dividend return is included because On does not currently pay one.

- FY2026 public anchors are CHF3.51 billion revenue and CHF1.41 adjusted/non-GAAP EPS as of September 3, 2026.
- FY2027 public headlines are CHF4.17 billion revenue and CHF1.68 adjusted EPS. Detailed public low/high tables were gated.

- Operating and net income are normalized scenario measures, not management guidance.
- FCF margins move toward 9% bear, 15% base, and 18% bull. FCF yield equals scenario FCF divided by scenario market capitalization.
- Diluted shares reach 350 million bear, 340 million base, and 335 million bull by FY2035.
- Terminal P/E anchors are 15x, 20x, and 25x normalized diluted EPS. Interim prices use a constant CAGR from $27.99 to terminal value.
- Revenue growth is year over year for the first three rows and annualized from the preceding displayed row for years five and ten.
