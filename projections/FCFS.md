The bear case assumes gold falls back toward $3,000 during 2027. Scrap gross profit halves from its Q3 run rate, average U.S. and U.K. loan sizes fall 15-20%, and retail margins compress for a year as inventory lent against at higher gold values is sold. AFF keeps shrinking. FY2027 revenue falls 2% to $4.27 billion and adjusted EPS slips to $10.33, below the FY2026 consensus. After FY2031 revenue grows 2% a year. The market treats FirstCash as a leveraged subprime lender and pays 9x by FY2036. The FCF yield climbs above 11% because the price falls while a shrinking pawn book releases cash; that is the signature of a permanently discounted stock rather than a bargain.

## Read

- The spread is wide, $136 to $561 in FY2036, and moderately skewed upward. The bear case loses 3.0% a year before dividends, the base case gains 6.7% a year and the bull case 11.7%.
- Gold is the swing variable. The difference between the bear and base FY2027 EPS ($10.33 vs $12.30) is mostly scrap profit and loan sizes, not pawn demand.
- The base case's one-year price ($197.86) is well below the street's $247.50 average target; this sheet is more conservative than the sell side in the near term because it assumes part of the gold windfall reverses.
- The base case return of 6.7% a year plus a 1% dividend is roughly the cost of equity, consistent with the Watch / Positive bias stance in the report.
- Price CAGRs exclude dividends. The $1.84 annualized dividend adds about 1.0 percentage point a year at today's price.

## Key Assumptions

- "Net Income" is adjusted net income (GAAP net income plus after-tax acquired-intangible amortization and merger costs), consistent with the adjusted EPS that consensus and the workbook use. GAAP net income runs roughly $45-$60 million a year lower.
- "Operating Income" is an approximate GAAP operating income at an assumed margin (bear 15.0%-16.0%, base 16.6%-17.5%, bull 17.0%-18.5%) against 16.4% TTM.
- "FCF Yield" is adjusted free cash flow (after net pawn-loan and finance-receivable fundings) divided by projected market cap. Adjusted FCF is assumed at 100% of adjusted net income in the bear case (a shrinking pawn book releases cash), 85% in the base case and 80% in the bull case (faster loan growth absorbs cash). TTM adjusted FCF was 85% of adjusted net income.
- Share counts: bear 43.0 million (FY2027) to 40.5 million (FY2036); base 42.6 million to 39.0 million; bull 42.4 million to 37.5 million.
- FY2026 base year: revenue $4,360 million and adjusted EPS $10.99, the consensus, in all cases.
- Terminal prices are FY2036 adjusted EPS × the stated terminal P/E (9.0x / 13.5x / 15.0x). Interim prices are walked back to $185.35 at a constant annual return. FY2031 prices land 7.9% above, 2.1% below and 19.5% below the workbook's bear, base and bull five-year targets, as explained above.
- Dividends are excluded from stock prices and CAGRs.
