## Read

- The scenario spread is driven more by FFO and P/FFO than by GAAP net income. Apartment depreciation keeps the displayed GAAP P/E near 28-34x even when the stock is reasonably valued on FFO.
- The bear is a prolonged-supply case, not a recession. It produces a price below the current quote, satisfying scenario discipline while preserving a viable operating business.
- The base case is close to sell-side fair value. Its expected return depends heavily on the dividend because price CAGR is only 2.1%.
- The bull case is operationally demanding but not fantastical: 4% revenue growth requires both rental recovery and successful development. It does not assume the 13.6% revenue growth of FY2022 returns.
- Price CAGRs exclude dividends. If the $6.12 distribution remains intact and grows slowly, total returns can exceed the displayed price returns by roughly 4-5 percentage points annually before tax and reinvestment effects.

## Key Assumptions

- FY2026 is anchored to live public data: $2.23 billion consensus revenue, $8.50 consensus FFO/share and MAA’s $8.53 Core FFO / $7.50 Core AFFO midpoints.
- FY2027 public revenue of $2.28 billion and adjusted EPS of $3.89 are visible, but detailed FY2027 FFO is gated. The base FY2027 revenue of $2.285 billion closely matches that public anchor; bear and bull bracket it.
- FY2028 through FY2036 are extrapolated. No hidden analyst estimates are used.
- Operating margins recover from 26.9% TTM toward 26% / 29.5% / 32% in bear/base/bull by FY2036. Net margins reach 17% / 20% / 23%.
- Shares and OP units drift to 121 million in bear, remain near 118 million in base and decline to 117 million in bull. This explicitly captures the risk that ATM issuance offsets buybacks.
- “Core AFFO Yield” is projected Core AFFO per share divided by projected stock price. It replaces conventional FCF yield because MAA’s real-estate acquisition/development spending is not maintenance capex, while company-defined Core AFFO deducts recurring capital expenditures.
- Terminal stock prices are first calculated from the workbook’s year-five Core FFO and P/FFO framework ($100.80 / $145.00 / $182.40), then interim prices are walked backward from each FY2036 endpoint to $117.78 at a constant annualized price-return path.
- The displayed terminal GAAP P/E anchors are outputs of terminal price divided by projected GAAP EPS. They are explicitly stated because `projections/README.md` requires terminal P/E anchoring, but P/FFO remains the correct REIT interpretation.
- Dividends are excluded from stock prices and CAGRs. A dividend cut, tax effect or reinvestment return is not modeled.
