## Read

- The bear case is a "mature platform, no optionality" scenario — revenue grows at 8% (device forensics penetration in remaining markets) but the Corellium acquisition fails to generate material cross-sell, AI forensics remains niche, and the market assigns a 10x FCF multiple appropriate for a low-growth specialized software business. The 1-year target of $8.70 (down 17% from current) reflects multiple contraction from the current 17.6x EV/FCF toward 10x. The 10-year CAGR of +5.5% is modest but positive — the stock is not worthless in this scenario, it just appreciates slowly.
- The base case is the scenario model's most probable outcome: 12% revenue CAGR bridging the gap between the 20%+ historical pace and the 8% bear reality. The key assumption is that the guide cut was a conservative restatement, not a growth death knell. At 15x FCF, the stock trades at sector-average multiples for 12% growth software. The 10-year CAGR of +15.0% is commensurate with the revenue growth rate plus modest multiple stability.
- The bull case is the "Corellium synergy + AI forensics mainstream" thesis — revenue reaches $2.87B in 10 years at 15% CAGR, operating margins expand to 20%+, and the market assigns a 20x FCF premium for a diversified digital investigations platform. The 10-year CAGR of +19.0% reflects both earnings growth and multiple expansion toward growth-software norms.
- FY2027 data points are extrapolated from the research model scenario parameters. All projections beyond 1 year represent the research analyst's best estimate of operating leverage trajectories — there is zero sell-side consensus data to anchor against for CLBT.
- The FCF Yield column shows terminal FCF divided by market cap. Higher yields indicate stronger cash generation relative to valuation.
- Net cash of $419.3M is treated as a constant addition to equity value in all scenarios. In reality, cash will be deployed for buybacks, acquisitions, or invested — the scenarios assume cash is reinvested at returns consistent with the case assumptions.
- The 10-year CAGR figures are arithmetic averages from the starting price of $10.44 to the terminal price — not annualized returns compounded with interim volatility. Actual investor returns would differ based on timing of entry, interim earnings surprises, capital allocation events, and macro volatility.
- GAAP earnings (net income) are shown for completeness but are not the primary valuation denominator. The non-operating volatility pattern (FY2024: -$331.5M, FY2023: -$109M) means trailing P/E is structurally misleading. FCF is the reliable earnings proxy for Cellebrite.
