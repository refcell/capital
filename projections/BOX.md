## Current Run-Rate

Box generated $1.234 billion of TTM revenue through July 2026, up 9.6%, with $346.3 million of company-defined non-GAAP FCF (28.1% margin) and $389.2 million of OCF less capex. Q2 FY2027 revenue was $321.1 million, up 9% reported and 11% in constant currency; non-GAAP operating margin was 29.4%; NRR was 106% (up from 103% a year earlier); billings grew 17%; and RPO reached $1.69 billion (+15%). These figures support a FY2027 base near $1.29 billion and a starting non-GAAP operating margin of about 28%.

Owner economics are weaker than the headline figures. TTM SBC was $235.7 million (19.1% of revenue, 68% of non-GAAP FCF), and repurchases of $460.7 million exceeded FCF. The KKR Series A preferred converts into about 18.57 million shares at $27.00 and is deep in the money. All per-share figures below therefore use a fully diluted share count that includes the preferred as-converted (about 159.6 million in FY2027: 141 million diluted common plus 18.6 million preferred), and normalized net income is non-GAAP net income before the preferred allocation. On that basis FY2027 normalized EPS is about $1.50, slightly below the company's two-class $1.54.

## Scenario Read

The one-year range is narrow: $41.28 bear, $45.60 base and $49.02 bull. All three FY2028 rows imply 26-27x normalized EPS, close to today's multiple, because the near-term debate is about growth durability rather than FY2028 earnings. The visible analyst target range of $26-$50 is wider than this one-year path and captures the same disagreement in multiple terms.

Long-term outcomes diverge because growth persistence, SBC and share count compound. In the base case Box grows revenue at 8.5% per year for ten years and fully diluted EPS at about 12.3% per year from $1.50 to $4.80, yet the stock compounds only 5.2% because the P/E compresses from 28x to 15x. That compression is the main reason the stance is Watch: at $43.34 the stock already discounts several years of above-trend execution. The bull case's 13.1% annual return requires low-teens growth sustained well past the suite-migration cycle. The bear case's -4.8% does not require revenue declines; it only requires growth to fade to low single digits and the market to value SBC-heavy earnings like a mature vendor.

## Key Assumptions

- FY2027 revenue is anchored at approximately $1.290 billion, consistent with Box's August 25, 2026 guide and StockAnalysis consensus.
- FY2028 through FY2037 are extrapolated beyond visible public estimates. No gated data is represented as public consensus. Year-five revenue in each case matches the workbook's FY2032 terminal revenue (bear $1.73B, base $2.08B, bull $2.38B).
- Operating income is non-GAAP, excluding SBC, acquired-intangible amortization and other special items, matching management's guidance basis. FY2027 non-GAAP operating margin is 28%.
- Normalized net income equals about 66.5-67% of non-GAAP operating income, calibrated to the FY2027 guide: $1.54 non-GAAP EPS plus $0.16 preferred allocation, multiplied by 141 million diluted shares, equals about $240 million, or 66.5% of $361 million non-GAAP operating income. It is not GAAP net income and excludes deferred-tax valuation allowance releases.
- Fully diluted shares include 18.57 million preferred shares as-converted. Bear declines from about 159.6 million (FY2027) to 148 million by FY2037; base to 130 million; bull to 122 million. The 2029 notes are treated as debt because the capped call offsets dilution up to $66.86.
- FCF margins use Box's non-GAAP FCF definition (after capitalized software): bear 27%; base 29% rising to 31%; bull 30% rising to 34%.
- Interim stock prices follow a constant annualized path between the October 29, 2026 price and each explicit FY2037 terminal price. They are valuation paths, not twelve-month targets.
- Revenue growth is year over year for consecutive displayed years; FY2032 and FY2037 growth figures are annualized from the preceding displayed year.
- FCF yield equals projected FCF divided by projected fully diluted market capitalization (scenario share count × scenario stock price).
- FX is held constant after FY2027. About 25% of revenue is yen-denominated, so a sustained 10% yen move would shift revenue by about 2.5 points.
