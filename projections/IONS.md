## Base Projection

Terminal P/E anchor: **18x FY2036 adjusted EPS of $8.00**, producing a year-10 stock price of **$144**. The base begins with the visible FY2027 public revenue headline of $1.38 billion and adjusted EPS of -$1.11. Revenue grows 11% annually as TRYNGOLZA, DAWNZERA and ZANVASTRO scale, WAINUA royalties rise, and selected pipeline programs offset a gradual SPINRAZA decline.

Operating margin improves from -12% in FY2027 to 22% in FY2036. Shares rise toward 180 million because the company requires additional financing before becoming self-funding, but dilution slows after FCF turns positive. An 18x terminal P/E is reasonable for a profitable specialty-biopharma company with a replenishing pipeline, but it is lower than the multiple often assigned during the pre-profitability option phase.

Stock prices work backward from $144 at a constant 12.1% annual path. The P/E column is not meaningful until EPS becomes positive; early prices should be read as a smooth terminal-value interpolation rather than year-specific price targets.

## Bull Projection

Terminal P/E anchor: **22x FY2036 adjusted EPS of $15.00**, producing a year-10 stock price of **$330**. The bull begins with $1.55 billion of FY2027 revenue, above the visible public headline, and grows 16% annually. TRYNGOLZA expands successfully beyond FCS, DAWNZERA wins meaningful HAE share, ZANVASTRO finds diagnosed patients efficiently, and multiple partnered programs reach the market.

Operating margin improves from -5% to 30%. Diluted shares rise only modestly toward 175 million because milestone receipts and early profitability replace external financing. The terminal 22x multiple assumes a durable rare-disease portfolio and continued pipeline productivity; it does not assume a broad-primary-care pharmaceutical margin structure.

Stock prices work backward from $330 at a constant 21.8% annual path. This is substantially above the current analyst-high target because it is a ten-year outcome, not a 12-month target.

## Read

The bear demonstrates that approved medicines do not create a hard valuation floor. If the commercial portfolio remains subscale and financing continues, a business with rising revenue can still deliver a negative stock-price CAGR. The $30 terminal value is 12 times positive EPS; the downside comes from weak earnings power, not an assumed zero.

The base produces a 12.1% annual stock-price CAGR before any dividend, which Ionis does not pay. That outcome is attractive but relies on moving from -$1.11 adjusted EPS in FY2027 to $8.00 by FY2036. The important inflection is FCF positivity around FY2029-FY2031, not the terminal multiple.

The bull's 21.8% CAGR requires a genuinely transformed company. Revenue must exceed $5.8 billion, margins must approach mature biopharma levels, and issuance must slow sharply. It is an upside scenario, not a planning assumption.

Across all cases, monitor retained product sales, royalty durability, annual cash use and diluted shares. Consolidated revenue alone is insufficient because milestone timing can create hundreds of millions of dollars of volatility without changing the underlying launch trajectory.

## Key Assumptions

The workbook uses forward EV/sales as its primary lens because both GAAP EPS and FCF are negative. This projection uses terminal P/E only to satisfy the ten-year Value Line-style format: each terminal price equals explicit FY2036 adjusted EPS multiplied by an explicit terminal P/E. Intermediate stock prices are geometric paths from the September 22 close to those terminal values.

The EPS figures are adjusted scenario values and should not be compared directly with GAAP history. FY2026 and FY2027 public anchors are also provider-specific non-GAAP adjusted figures. Net income in the tables equals scenario EPS multiplied by scenario diluted shares.

FCF yield equals scenario FCF divided by scenario equity capitalization. Long-range FCF is an extrapolation because detailed public estimates were unavailable. The bear assumes continued negative FCF through the middle of the horizon, the base turns positive around FY2029, and the bull turns positive in FY2028.

No dividend is assumed. Gross debt is not explicitly amortized in the tables; its cost is reflected in net income, FCF and scenario multiples. No separate value is added for unapproved programs. Their expected contribution appears only when it raises scenario revenue or earnings.
