## Read

The bear case is a 2027-jump-off case, not a revenue-collapse case. Sales still sit near $175–188B, and the company still earns ~$4 of adjusted EPS, but 2026’s $0.50 of extras do not repeat, Medicaid acuity spends the HBR cushion, and the multiple goes to 10x. Year-1 price is $66.29, 5% below the quote on the constant-CAGR path; the workbook bear of $46.20 is the five-year checkpoint if that path is marked to a 10.5x exit sooner. That is the required shape: a bear case with downside. Ten-year CAGR is −4.9%. Capital is slowly given back, not wiped out, because premium still throws off ~1.5% FCF.

The base case is the Street case. FY2027 uses the last public consensus ($195.32B / $5.55). Earnings grind to $6.05 by FY2031 and $6.80 by FY2036. Terminal 12.5x is mid-cycle managed care. Year-5 workbook target $75.62 is the $75 average PT. Ten-year price $85.00 is a 2.0% CAGR from $69.74, well below WACC of 8.14% and below cost of equity of 10.26%. This is a fair stock, not a cheap one. The 2% price CAGR versus 8% WACC is the restoration leftover: ROE is still below Ke, so book premium does not compound.

The bull case requires 2027 to grow through $5.20, not just through $4.70. That is a February 2027 operating outcome, not a November 2026 multiple outcome. Even then the terminal P/E is 13.5x, not 18x. Year-5 $87.75 (workbook) and year-10 $99.90 are the upside if MA, Marketplace, and Medicaid HBR all cooperate. They are not a re-rating back to a quality-compounder multiple.
