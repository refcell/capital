The bear case assumes capacity for 2027 slips and GPU rental pricing resets once NVIDIA supply catches up. FY2027 revenue of $10 billion is 18% below consensus, and revenue then grows only 5-9% a year as renewals of 2025-2026 vintage capacity reprice sharply lower. GAAP operating margin stays below 13% because depreciation on an oversized fleet eats most of a 45% EBITDA margin, and interest on about $18 billion of net debt keeps net income negative until FY2030. Shares rise to 345 million as equity is sold at low prices. The market values the survivor at 12x earnings by FY2036. The FY2031 P/E of 54.9x is a path artifact: earnings are just turning positive while the constant-return price is still falling. The FCF yield turns high late in the period because the build stops and the price is low, which is the signature of a distressed, ex-growth GPU landlord rather than a margin of safety.

## Read

- The spread is very wide, $36.82 to $965.56 in FY2036, and the outcome depends far more on GPU pricing and funding costs after 2027 than on the 2026-2027 numbers, which are largely contracted.
- The bear case loses 13.3% a year; the base case earns 12.2% a year, almost exactly the 12.28% CAPM cost of equity in the workbook; the bull case earns 20.2% a year. The stock is roughly fairly priced on the base case and needs bull-case execution to produce excess returns.
- All three cases show negative FCF yield through FY2029. Price support in that period comes from revenue and EBITDA growth and access to capital, not from cash returns.
- The base case one-year price of $171.98 is well below the $281 street average target: this sheet is more cautious than the sell side near term because funding terms, not demand, are the binding constraint into 2027.
- Nebius pays no dividend, so price CAGR is total return.

## Key Assumptions

- Revenue is consolidated group revenue; the AI cloud was about 98% of Q2 2026 revenue. FY2026 is the $3.35 billion consensus in all cases and is the base for FY2027 growth.
- Operating income is GAAP, after depreciation of GPUs and servers over five years (the useful life Nebius adopted in 2026) and after share-based compensation. Adjusted EBITDA margins in the workbook (45% / 55% / 57% in FY2031) are higher by depreciation and SBC.
- Net income equals operating income less interest (bear $0.7-1.1 billion, base $1.0-2.3 billion, bull $1.1-2.9 billion a year), taxed at 21% when positive and with no tax benefit when negative. Equity-stake gains, which made GAAP net income positive in FY2025 and TTM, are excluded.
- FCF ($ billions) is assumed at −9 / −4 / −1 / +1.5 / +2.5 (bear), −12 / −9 / −5 / +2 / +9 (base) and −15 / −14 / −9 / +1 / +18 (bull) for FY2027, FY2028, FY2029, FY2031 and FY2036. FCF yield is FCF divided by projected price times projected shares.
- Share counts: bear 300 million (FY2027) to 345 million (FY2036); base 295 million to 335 million; bull 290 million to 315 million. Dilution comes from the ATM program, convertible notes and SBC.
- Terminal prices are FY2036 EPS × the stated terminal P/E (12.0x / 20.0x / 22.0x). Interim prices are walked back to $153.27 at a constant annual return. FY2031 prices land 9.7% above the workbook bear target and 14.5% and 29.4% below the base and bull targets, as explained above.
- The ClickHouse and Toloka stakes (carried within $1.62 billion of long-term investments) are not included in any case.
