## Read

- The bear case is an earnings disappointment scenario: operating margin continues to decline as lower-margin acquisitions dilute the core, "Other income/expense" charges recur, and the forward P/E of 6.9x proves to be the peak, not the floor. The stock slowly grinds down toward the $225-$275 range.
- The base case follows analyst consensus through FY27, then assumes stable operating income with EPS growth primarily driven by continued share repurchases. The total return from price appreciation is modest (~6.3% CAGR) — the value unlock is in the correction of the earnings quality overhang.
- The bull case requires a genuine margin recovery story: every new dollar of revenue generates more operating profit than the dollar it replaced. This would require integration synergies that haven't materialized yet, or an improvement in aftermarket service margins. The multiple re-rating to 10.5x P/E converges toward the trailing average.
- All three cases assume share count continues declining via buybacks (estimated 3-4% annually through FY28, then tapering), which is the primary mechanism for EPS growth above organic operating income growth.
- The FCF yield column is illustrative only — FCF multiples are not the primary valuation framework for GPI due to FCF insufficiency relative to net debt. FCF yields of 1-3% reflect thin conversion from revenue.

## Key Assumptions

- Base and bear FY26-FY27 revenue and EPS figures are anchored in visible Yahoo Finance analyst consensus ($22.79B/$42.27 FY26, $23.53B/$47.49 FY27). FY2028+ are extrapolated at 2-4% annual revenue growth consistent with the auto dealership sector.
- Bull FY26-FY27 revenue slightly exceeds consensus ($23.8B vs $23.53B for FY27), reflecting the upside scenario.
- FY2029 through FY2036 are extrapolated because visible public analyst estimates are not available beyond FY2027. Growth rates are set consistent with dealer group historical norms (2-4% for mature platforms).
- Net income figures are derived from projected operating income less projected interest expense and taxes. Interest expense is assumed to stabilize near $290-320M in bear/base and decline modestly in bull. Tax rate assumed at 28%.
- Share count assumed to decline by 3-4% per year through FY28 via continued buybacks, then stabilize. Current 11.9M shares declining toward 10M.
- EPS is computed as projected net income divided by projected share count. FY26 EPS of $42.27 and FY27 of $47.49 are the Yahoo Finance analyst anchors.
- FCF yield uses projected free cash flow (estimated as 1-3% of revenue) divided by projected market capitalization at the projected stock price.
- Stock prices work backward from a year-10 terminal P/E anchor: 7.5x bear, 9.0x base, and 10.5x bull. Interim prices follow a constant CAGR path from $296.81 to the terminal value.
- The 10-year CAGR values (-0.9%, +6.3%, +7.5%) are the price-only return. The stock has a negligible dividend yield (0.76% forward) — adding dividend reinvestment adds approximately 0.75% to total return.
- P/E is calculated as projected stock price divided by projected EPS. Trailing P/E of 10.96x is the upper bound for the bull case.
