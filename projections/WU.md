## Read

- The spread is very wide: $2.90 to $21.76 in FY2036. At 5x earnings, small changes in the direction of EPS dominate the outcome. Multiple expansion only happens if earnings stop falling.
- The bear case produces downside every year. That keeps the scenarios honest: a 5x P/E is not a floor when earnings are shrinking 5%-10% a year.
- The base case is close to sell-side fair value on a 12-month view. Its value to shareholders is almost entirely the dividend, which would add roughly 14 percentage points a year at today's price if it is maintained.
- The bull case does not require a return to revenue growth beyond acquisitions and low-single-digit organic gains. It requires cost and payout repricing to outrun the mix shift.
- Price CAGRs exclude dividends. The $0.94 dividend equals 14.6% of today's price; a cut is assumed only in the bear case.

## Key Assumptions

- FY2026 is anchored to the $4.06 billion consensus revenue and an adjusted EPS of about $1.27, near the low end of the $1.25-$1.35 guide.
- Adjusted net income equals (revenue × adjusted operating margin − interest and other expense) × (1 − 14%). The 14% rate matches the Q2 2026 adjusted effective tax rate. Interest and other expense rises from ~$147 million TTM to $165-$180 million in base and bear cases with Intermex funding, then declines as debt is repaid.
- "Adj. Operating Income" and "Adj. Net Income" are non-GAAP projections consistent with the adjusted EPS framework used by management and consensus. GAAP figures will be lower by acquisition amortization, restructuring and integration costs.
- Shares stay at 312 million in the bear case, fall to 300 million in the base case and 270 million in the bull case. The buyback was paused on July 30, 2026, so declines before FY2028 are small.
- FCF yield is company-defined FCF (after capex and capitalized signing bonuses and software) per share divided by the projected price. Bear / base / bull FCF is $280M / $420M / $560M in FY2031.
- Terminal prices are calculated from FY2036 EPS × the stated terminal P/E, then interim prices are walked back to $6.43 at a constant annual price return. FY2031 prices land within about 3.5% of the workbook's five-year targets of $4.25 / $7.15 / $12.25.
- Dividends are excluded from stock prices and CAGRs.
