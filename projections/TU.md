## Read

- The bear case is a value-trap scenario: revenue stagnates, capex reaccelerates, debt continues growing, and the market prices Telus as a declining telecom with a looming dividend cut. The 10.0x terminal P/E reflects this deterioration.
- The base case follows analyst consensus closely through FY2027, then assumes modest compounding of 3-4% as the capex cycle fully transitions and FCF stabilizes. The total return including dividend is the primary value driver — approximately 10-11% annually.
- The bull case is a cyclical recovery thesis: the capex trough becomes a clear FCF peak, debt is incrementally reduced, and the stock re-rates. The short squeeze at 37.5% drawdown from peak adds mechanical upside.
- All three cases assume stable CAD/USD exchange. Currency moves would shift USD-valued EPS and prices proportionally.
- Revenue figures are in CAD throughout; EPS, stock price, and market-cap-derived metrics are in USD.

## Key Assumptions

- Base and bear FY2026-FY2027 revenue figures are anchored in visible Yahoo Finance analyst consensus ($20.77B FY26, $21.25B FY27 CAD). FY2028+ are extrapolated at 2-3% annual growth.
- Bull FY2026-FY2027 revenue slightly exceeds consensus (21.5B vs 21.25B for FY27), reflecting the upside scenario.
- FY2029 through FY2036 are extrapolated because visible public analyst estimates are not available beyond FY2027. Growth rates are set consistent with Canadian telecom historical norms (1-3% for mature carriers).
- EPS figures are derived from projected net income divided by a constant 1.6B shares (slight further dilution assumption for M&A through the horizon). The FY2026 EPS of $0.68 and FY2027 of $0.72 are the Yahoo Finance analyst anchors.
- FCF yield uses projected free cash flow (estimated as 10-12% of revenue in base/bull, 5-7% in bear) divided by projected market capitalization at the projected stock price.
- Stock prices work backward from a year-10 terminal P/E anchor: 10.0x bear, 16.0x base, and 20.0x bull. Interim prices follow a constant CAGR path from $10.19 to the terminal value.
- The 10-year CAGR values (-10.8%, +2.9%, +13.7%) are the price-only return, excluding dividend reinvestment. Adding the projected dividend yield of ~5-7% would add approximately 5-7 percentage points to total return in the base/bull cases.
- P/E is calculated as projected stock price divided by projected USD EPS (assuming 20.0x for bull year 10, 16.0x for base, 10.0x for bear).
