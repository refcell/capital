## Bear Projection

The bear case assumes the operating income decline is structural, not transitional. Post-spinoff, Solventum's $8.3B revenue base supports a higher operating expense rate than 3M's $30B platform ever required. Q2 beat of 33.78% is a base effect — conservative estimates followed by modest operational results. Q3 disappoints, EPS falls below $1.80. The multiple compresses from 12.7x toward 10x as the market concludes the $1.69B → $526M operating income decline is permanent. HIS separation removes $1B+ of revenue. Revenue base shrinks to ~$7.5B. No organic growth. No dividend. No buybacks. Per-share value stagnates.

## Extrapolation Notes

- Revenue growth rates for Years 2-10 in all scenarios are extrapolated beyond visible public analyst estimates. Yahoo Finance analyst consensus covers FY2026 and FY2027 only.
- Post-HIS-separation revenue base is estimated. The HIS segment contributed approximately $1B+ of revenue. The base/bull projections implicitly assume organic Medsurg/Dental growth partially offsets the HIS revenue loss.
- Operating margin recovery trajectories are the largest model assumption. The 4-year trend of decline (20.8% → 6.3%) has no historical precedent for recovery speed.
- FCF yield in early years is low or negative because OCF has collapsed (from $1.68B in FY22 to $209M TTM). Recovery of OCF is a prerequisite for meaningful FCF yield.
- The terminal P/E anchors (10x bear, 13x base, 16x bull) are calibrated against medtech peer P/E norms. SOLV is a medtech company with no clinical trial binary or regulatory cliff.
- Share count is assumed flat at 173.49M shares through all projections. If HIS proceeds fund buybacks, actual share count would be lower, boosting per-share metrics and stock price.
- Net debt trajectory: Base case assumes annualized $1.5B+ debt reduction continues, reaching ~$2B net debt by FY29.
