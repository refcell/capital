## Read

- The bear case is a structural capex and margin-compression story. If the Q3 FY26 disaster quarter (0.29% net margin) proves to be the new baseline rather than an outlier, operating income stabilizes at $740-820M on $17-18B revenue (4-5% op margin), producing $427-470M in net income. At 8.0x forward P/E and stable share count (no buybacks), the stock falls toward $18-20. The 10-year price target of $16 is consistent with a cyclical-manufacturing multiple (6-7x at peak compression) and modest revenue decline. The 10-year CAGR of -16.6% represents a full earnings-cycle unwind — the same magnitude as the 52-week decline from $50 to $26.86.

- The bear revenue trajectory (-1.1% to -1.3% CAGR) assumes commodity protein market share erosion and feed cost headwinds prevent even index-level growth. This is consistent with the FY23-25 pattern where revenue was essentially flat across three fiscal years ($17.36B → $17.88B → $18.50B).

- The base case anchors FY2027 at $18.7B revenue (+1.4%) and $760M operating income (7.0% margin) — a partial recovery from the TTM trough but below the FY24-25 peak. The 1.5-2% revenue CAGR reflects the mature protein market: PPC has already achieved the scale it can reasonably capture in the US chicken and pork markets. The $37.50 target at 10x P/E on $3.75 EPS aligns with analyst consensus and represents the probability-weighted most likely outcome.

- The bull case is a capex-cycle-recovery-and-margin-rebound story. If the Q1 FY27 earnings on October 28 show capex below $220M for the quarter (from TTM $230M/quarter) and net margin above 2%, the narrative resets from "structural deterioration" to "transitional trough." The 13x P/E multiple would represent the upper bound for a food processor with demonstrated capacity to earn 8.9%+ operating margins (FY24-FY25 track record). Revenue grows 4% CAGR from prepared-food mix improvement and moderate pricing power.

## Key Assumptions

- Forward P/E of 7.17x derived from Yahoo Key Stats. Implied consensus EPS of $3.75/year for the forward year. This is the only explicit EPS anchor available — Yahoo analysis page shows revenue estimates but no EPS low/avg/high breakdown.

- TTM FCF of $304M is a trough number from an elevated capex cycle ($917M TTM vs $711M FY25 vs $476M FY24). The FCF framework was not used as the primary valuation lens. Forward P/E is primary; FCF yield appears in the projection tables but is secondary and illustrative.

- Capex normalization assumptions: Bear assumes permanent elevation at $800-900M/year. Base assumes recovery to $600-700M (FY25 levels). Bull assumes return to $450-500M (FY23-24 levels). The capex trajectory is the single most important variable in the projection.

- Operating margin assumptions: Bear 4-5% structural compression. Base 7-8% partial recovery. Bull 9-10% near FY24-25 peak (8.9%).

- Effective tax rate assumed at 25% for FY2027+ projections (below TTM's 29.1% which reflects the low-earnings base distortion). FY24-25 showed tax rates of 20-23%.

- Share count held constant at 238.11M across all cases. PPC has shown no buyback activity TTM and no dividend. JBS parent maintains 82% insider control. Per-share calculations use the existing diluted count; no additional dilution assumed.

- The FCF framework was not the primary valuation lens because TTM FCF ($304M) is a capex-cycle trough number. Using a 10-12x FCF multiple on $304M would produce EV of $3-3.6B, implying stock prices of $5-10, which are economically meaningless for an $18B+ revenue company.

- Interest expense held at $165M/year based on 4-year stability ($161-165M across FY22-FY25-TTM), implying a predominantly fixed-rate debt book.

- Revenue growth is YoY from the prior fiscal year for consecutive displayed years. For FY2031 and FY2036 displayed years, the growth rate is an annualized approximation between the nearest displayed intervals.

- Terminal P/E anchors are 8.0x bear (cyclical manufacturing norms for a commoditized protein producer with structural margin issues), 10.0x base (food processor steady-state with demonstrated 7-8% op margin capacity consistent with analyst consensus), and 13.0x bull (premium multiple for a proven $1B+ earnings producer with full margin recovery). Peer comparison: SFD ~8-10x, TSN ~12-15x, HRL ~15-18x.

- FY2028, FY2029, FY2031, and FY2036 rows are extrapolated beyond visible public estimates. They are anchored to the research note (research/PPC.md) scenario model and the 6-sheet workbook (models/[2026-08-12] Pilgrim's Pride Model.xlsx) terminal-year math.

- The 10-year CAGR figures represent the annualized compound rate from the starting price of $26.86 to the terminal price. Actual investor returns would differ based on timing, interim earnings surprises, and macro volatility.
