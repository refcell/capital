## Read

- The bear case assumes Tanger enters a flat-growth phase: revenue compounds at 2% CAGR (lease rollover at current rates), same-store rental income stagnates, and the market compresses P/E as the FFO multiple declines toward 28x. The declining P/E from 38x toward terminal levels reflects the market re-evaluating Tanger as a flat-growth REIT. Adding the ~3% dividend yield, total bear return is roughly -5% annualized.
- The base case assumes steady 3-4% revenue growth, stable 30% operating margin, and gradual P/E normalization toward low-30s on EPS by year 10. The dividend yield of ~3% adds roughly 3 percentage points to the annualized total return, bringing base case total return to ~7.3% annualized. Note that the terminal P/E of 33x on EPS runs high because REIT depreciation inflates GAAP EPS distortion; the equivalent P/FFO would be roughly 18x, which is fair.
- The bull case requires consumer trade-down tailwinds, lease rollover at above-market rates, and potential share count reduction through buybacks. Revenue compounds at 5-6% CAGR, operating margin expands toward 32%, and FFO growth accelerates. With dividends, total bull return is ~14.4% annualized.
- The stock-price CAGR figures shown in each table exclude dividends. SKT's 3% dividend yield should be added to estimate total return.
- Terminal P/E anchors are on normalized EPS. Because Tanger is a REIT and GAAP earnings are depressed by $154M+/year of depreciation, the displayed P/E figures are elevated (28-38x range) relative to non-REIT comparables. The P/FFO equivalent is roughly 16-22x, which is more comparable to REIT peer valuations.

## Key Assumptions

- Public estimate visibility is limited to FY2026 and FY2027 low/average/high revenue and EPS from Yahoo Finance (14 analysts). FY2028 through FY2036 are model extrapolations informed by the local workbook scenario assumptions (2% / 4% / 6% revenue CAGR), the local research note's growth and margin framing, and the terminal P/E anchor framework required by projections/README.md.
- Revenue growth is YoY for consecutive displayed years. For FY2031 and FY2036, it represents the annualized revenue growth rate bridging from the prior displayed fiscal year.
- Operating income is assumed to track revenue with a roughly stable operating margin (bear: 28-30%, base: 30%, bull: 31-32%).
- Net income is a normalized proxy — it does not precisely match GAAP net income because REIT depreciation ($154M+ annually) distorts the GAAP figure. The net income shown is a "cash-equivalent earnings" proxy derived from operating income minus estimated tax/interest burden, which is why the P/E figures are elevated.
- Share count is assumed stable in base and bear, declining modestly in bull (buyback program assumption at 1-2%/year).
- FCF Yield is OCF divided by implied market capitalization at the projected stock price × shares outstanding. It shows the owner-earnings yield at each projected price level.
- Interim stock prices are walked back from each case's FY2036 terminal price to the current $42.11 price on a constant annualized price-return path, per projections/README.md.
- StockAnalysis returned 404 for SKT, so all consensus figures come from Yahoo Finance, which has more limited analyst coverage details than StockAnalysis. The local workbook in `models/[2026-07-28] Tanger Inc. Model.xlsx` contributes the scenario structure and the local research note in `research/SKT.md` contributes the qualitative framing for growth rates, multiples, and terminal anchors.
