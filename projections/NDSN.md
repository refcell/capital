## Read

- **The bear case** assumes Nordson reverts to its historical low-growth profile: revenue compounds at 2% CAGR (purely organic, no further acquisitions). The multiple compresses from the current 24.7x toward 18x — consistent with AMETEK's trailing multiple or a generic slow-growth industrial. The declining P/E from 24x toward 18x reflects the market re-evaluating Nordson as a 2% growth compounder rather than an acquisition-accelerated story. Even in bear, FCF yield holds in the 4-5% range, providing downside support. Bear price CAGR is +0.5% — effectively flat over 10 years.

- **The base case** assumes steady 4% revenue growth, driven by the expanded medical and advanced technology segments that the FY2023-2024 acquisitions created. Operating margin holds near 25%, net margin near 18%. FCF yield remains stable at 4.0% through the horizon. The P/E multiple remains at ~24x throughout — Nordson is neither cheap nor expensive, priced fairly for its quality. Price CAGR of +4.7% is driven by EPS compounding rather than multiple expansion. Adding the ~1.05% dividend yield, total base return is ~5.7% annualized.

- **The bull case** requires the medical and advanced technology segments to drive sustained 6-7% organic growth, with operating margins expanding to 27%+ from the current 26.8%. The multiple gently expands from ~24x toward 28x — approaching but not reaching ITW-level valuations (~35x). FCF yield compresses to 3.0% at the high end because earnings grow faster than the cash flow in absolute terms (margin expansion drives disproportionate EPS growth). Price CAGR of +8.6% driven by both EPS growth and modest multiple re-rating. With the 1.05% dividend yield, total bull return is ~9.7% annualized.

- The stock-price CAGR figures in each table exclude dividends. NDSN's ~1.05% dividend should be added to estimate total return.

- All three cases work backward from explicit year-10 terminal P/E anchors per projections/README.md guidance. The interim prices use constant CAGR paths from $309.99 to the terminal value. The FCF Yield column shows the annualized free cash flow yield as a percentage of implied market cap at each projected price level.

- Public estimate visibility is limited to FY2026 and FY2027 from Yahoo Finance Analysis (~15 analysts). - Revenue growth is YoY for consecutive displayed years. For FY2032 and FY2037, it represents the annualized revenue growth rate bridging from the prior displayed fiscal year.

- Share count is assumed stable in base and bear, declining modestly in bull (buyback program assumption at 1-2%/year).
- FCF Yield is OCF divided by implied market capitalization at the projected stock price times shares outstanding. It shows the owner-earnings yield at each projected price level.
- Interim stock prices are walked forward from the current $309.99 price to each case's FY2037 terminal price on a constant annualized price-return path, per projections/README.md.
- The forward P/E framework is used as the primary valuation method. NDSN is a profitable slow-growth industrial — the P/E framework is appropriate. FCF multiple was used in the local workbook as a cross-check only.
