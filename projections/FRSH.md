## Current Run-Rate

Freshworks generated $903.9 million of TTM revenue through June 2026, up 15.6%, with $233.8 million of reported FCF and a 25.9% FCF margin. Q2 revenue was $237.4 million, up 16%; non-GAAP operating margin was 23.6%; and adjusted FCF margin was 24.3%. These figures support a FY2026 base near $965 million and a starting adjusted margin in the low-to-mid 20s.

The run-rate is not uniformly strong. Net dollar retention was 104%, down from 106% sequentially and year over year. Larger customers are expanding faster: customers contributing more than $100,000 in ARR rose 25%, compared with 6% growth in customers above $5,000. The scenarios therefore differ more in long-run retention, enterprise mix, and AI monetization than in FY2026 revenue.

Reported FCF includes a $127.7 million TTM SBC add-back, while repurchases consumed $409.7 million. The projection tables show reported/adjusted FCF consistent with public data, but terminal P/E anchors are applied to normalized non-GAAP net income and the interpretation explicitly treats SBC as an owner cost. Share counts decline in the base and bull cases only because repurchases are assumed to continue at more sustainable levels.

## Scenario Read

The one-year range is deliberately narrower than the long-term range. The bear FY2027 revenue assumption of $1.03 billion is below the visible $1.10 billion headline consensus and implies a rapid deceleration. The base equals public consensus. The bull assumes that Q2's 25% growth in >$100,000 ARR customers and 24% EX ARR growth pull total growth above current expectations. At one year, much of the debate is already captured by the $10-$25 analyst target range.

Long-term outcomes diverge because retention and owner economics compound. At 104% NDR, Freshworks must keep winning new logos to sustain growth. A rise toward 110% materially reduces that burden and supports better margins. A fall toward 100% creates the opposite effect. SBC creates similar compounding: if equity compensation falls below 10% of revenue, reported FCF becomes much closer to owner cash; if it remains near 14%, higher reported FCF requires continuing repurchases.

The base 7.8% annual stock-price return is adequate but not exceptional for a company with execution and competitive risk. The bull's 16.6% requires evidence that is not yet present in the public numbers. The bear's negative return does not require insolvency or revenue decline; it only requires Freshworks to become a slower-growth vendor whose adjusted cash flow receives a lower multiple.

## Key Assumptions

- FY2026 revenue is anchored at approximately $965 million, consistent with both Freshworks' August 4 guide and StockAnalysis consensus.
- FY2027 base revenue of $1.10 billion and adjusted EPS context of $0.83 come from the visible StockAnalysis headline estimate. FY2027 bear and bull are scenario estimates, not public low/high forecasts.
- FY2028, FY2029, FY2031, and FY2036 are extrapolated beyond visible public estimates. No paid or gated data is represented as public consensus.
- Operating income is adjusted/non-GAAP because management guidance and visible EPS consensus exclude SBC, employer payroll taxes on equity awards, acquired-intangible amortization, restructuring, and acquisition expenses. The report separately treats SBC as an owner cost.
- Normalized net income uses scenario net margins and declining share counts. It is not GAAP net income and excludes the non-recurring deferred-tax benefit that inflated FY2025 and TTM results.
- Bear share count declines from 260.9 million to 250 million by FY2036; base declines to 235 million; bull declines to 225 million. Repurchases are assumed to become internally funded rather than remain at the TTM pace of $409.7 million.
- Bear FCF margin is 20%; base rises from 24.5% to 26.5%; bull rises from 26% to 30%. These are reported/adjusted cash-flow margins and must be read alongside SBC.
- Interim stock prices are calculated from a constant annualized path between the September 1, 2026 price and each explicit FY2036 terminal price. They are valuation paths, not twelve-month analyst targets.
- Revenue growth is year over year for consecutive displayed years. FY2031 and FY2036 growth figures are annualized from the preceding displayed year.
- FCF yield equals projected FCF divided by projected market capitalization using each scenario's projected share count and stock price.
