## Read

- The bear case is a cash-depletion scenario, not a business-failure scenario in the traditional sense. Revenue remains at zero for the decade as clinical trials fail to reach approval and the company exhausts its cash over multiple dilutive financing rounds. The terminal value of $5.00 per share reflects the remaining cash per share after 45% dilution, with the pipeline written off entirely. The 10-year CAGR of -23.8% is a terminal-erosion path: each financing round dilutes per-share NAV by roughly 20-25%, and the remaining cash generates insufficient interest income to offset the decline.

- The base case assumes at least one program (likely the Apogee PD-1 PD-1 or ORKA-002 anti-IL-17) triggers a licensing or partnership deal between FY2029 and FY2031, providing partial validation without requiring full Phase 3 + approval across the entire pipeline. The $35.00 terminal value reflects post-dilution NAV plus partial pipeline recognition. The 10-year CAGR of -19.7% still shows negative returns from current prices because the stock has already run hard off of the Apogee catalyst and the base case does not assume a full strategic exit.

- The bull case requires the Apogee PD-1 Phase 3 to show positive results, triggering strategic acquisition interest from mid-to-large pharma players. The $95.00 terminal value assumes a $9B+ company valuation at exit with only 20% dilution from strategic partnering rather than public market financing. Revenue of $800M by FY2036 assumes the licensed/partnered product reaches limited commercialization or generates milestone/royalty revenue. Unlike the bear and base cases, the bull CAGR is positive (+5.5%) because the acquisition/partnership premium offsets dilution. However, this outcome requires near-optimal execution across multiple clinical programs simultaneously — an outcome that historically occurs in <20% of clinical-stage biotechs.

- All three cases work backward from explicit year-10 terminal values per projections/README.md. The interim prices use constant CAGR paths from $85.21 to the terminal value. The FCF Yield column shows the annualized loss rate as a percentage of implied market cap — all cases are negative for most of the horizon, which is the defining characteristic of a clinical-stage biotech projection. P/E is N/A throughout because the company does not generate positive earnings until the bull case reaches FY2036.

## Key Assumptions

- Standard P/E-based projection framework is not applicable. ORKA has $0 revenue, negative EPS across all analyst forecasts through FY2027, and no positive FCF. The projections use the workbook's NAV + pipeline NPV framework with explicit terminal value anchors per the README format.
- Analyst estimates from Yahoo Finance provide FY2026 and FY2027 EPS anchors (all negative, as expected for a clinical-stage operator). Revenue estimates are all -- (no revenue visibility).
- Share count starts at ~60.3M (implied from July 2, 2026 intraday MC at $85.21). FY2025 basic average shares were 45.61M — the 32% increase is attributed to Apogee acquisition consideration. No attempt was made to obtain exact post-deal share count.
- Burn rate assumptions: bear $160M/year (accelerated post-Apogee), base $150M/year (moderately elevated), bull $140M/year (efficient integration). These are extrapolated from TTM burn of $133.4M and assume Apogee adds headcount and trial costs.
- Revenue assumptions: zero through initial years, then modest milestone/royalty/licensing revenue in the bull case only ($50M by FY2029, $800M by FY2036). Bear and base remain at zero because the programs fail or achieve licensing deals that generate minimal upfront revenue.
- Dilution factor: bear 1.45×, base 1.30×, bull 1.20×. These represent total cumulative dilution from all future financing rounds over the 10-year horizon.
- Terminal values: $5.00 bear (NAV only), $35.00 base (NAV + partial pipeline), $95.00 bull (strategic acquisition premium). These match the 6-sheet workbook Scenarios sheet.
- The 10-year Treasury rate is 4.485% (CNBC, Jul 2, 2026), which provides the cost-of-equity anchor for the NAV calculations.
- Revenue in FY2036 for the base and bear cases is assumed at zero because clinical-stage biotechs without successful Phase 3 readouts and regulatory approval typically remain pre-revenue throughout the clinical development cycle, which averages 8-12 years from Phase 2a to approval.
- The FCF Yield column is calculated as projected net loss divided by projected market capitalization (projected price × post-dilution shares). It is negative in all cases through most of the horizon, reflecting the structural cash requirements of clinical development.
