## Extrapolation Notes

- **FCF Yield is marked N/A** for all years because FCF is not a meaningful valuation metric for banks. Deposits offset loan origination; OCF exceeds net income on an accrual basis. The P/B + ROE framework replaces the standard revenue/FCF/earnings growth template.
- **P/B replaces P/E as the primary multiples metric** because banks trade on book value and ROE, not earnings multiples. The P/E column shows trailing implied P/E for comparison but is secondary.
- **FY27 revenue and EPS** are from analyst consensus (Yahoo Finance Analysis, 10 analysts, Non-GAAP diluted). FY26 revenue of $1.20B and EPS of $1.68 are also analyst consensus.
- **Years 3-10 are extrapolated** beyond the visible public estimate horizon. The growth rates (2.0%/5.0%/7.5% CAGR) are applied to the FY27 analyst anchor for each scenario.
- **BVPS compounding** uses the common equity base of $19.58 per share (Yahoo Finance MRQ). Retained earnings are estimated as ROE × Common Equity. The buyback effect is implicit in the BVPS CAGR for the base and bull cases.
- **Year-10 terminal P/B anchors** (0.70x bear, 1.10x base, 1.40x bull) are derived from the workbook scenario model exit P/B parameters with a slight additional expansion/compression for the 10-year horizon.
- **Stock prices work backward** from the terminal P/B anchor by computing the terminal BVPS and multiplying by the exit P/B ratio. Intermediate prices interpolate BVPS growth and gradual P/B movement toward the terminal anchor.
