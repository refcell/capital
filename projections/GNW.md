## Projection Notes

- Revenue projections are essentially flat to slightly declining in bear/base and slightly rising in bull. Genworth is a mature franchise with no organic growth runway. Revenue growth in the bull case is driven by mortgage insurance tailwinds (higher rates, larger LTVs, more MI required), which is cyclical not structural.
- Operating income is the key variable — it captures the transition from LTC unwind volatility toward stable MI + annuity earnings. The $485-605M range in FY27 narrows to $439-713M by FY36 as the unwind finishes and organic earnings dominate.
- Net income benefits from buyback compounding. With ~383M shares today and aggressive buybacks reducing the count by 1-2M shares annually, EPS compounds even on flat total net income.
- The FY2027 analyst estimate of $1.10 EPS (K.B.W.) anchors the year-1 projections. Beyond that, all figures are extrapolated from the research model.
- The terminal P/E anchors reflect insurance industry norms: 14x for a stagnant, no-re-rating name; 18x for a moderate re-rating to accept resolved LTC risk; 22x for a significant re-rating that essentially erases the AIG stigma. None of these are generous multiples.
- P/B anchors: Bear 0.40x (no change from current), Base 0.50-0.55x (modest improvement), Bull 0.65-0.70x (meaningful but still below peer median of 0.8x).
