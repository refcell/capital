## Read

- Bear is a genuine downside scenario. Its $90 year-5 target is 26.5% below the September 11 quote, and the path reaches $80.75 by year 10.
- Base follows the visible FY2027 and FY2028 public estimates, then uses 1.5% revenue growth. The $170.40 year-5 value is effectively equal to the $170.83 analyst average target.
- Bull requires volume recovery, not merely price/mix, tax benefits, or buybacks. Its year-5 target is above current sell-side targets and should remain a low-confidence outcome until depletion turns positive.
- Price paths work backward from explicit year-10 adjusted-P/E anchors and pass through the workbook’s year-5 targets. Price CAGRs exclude dividends.

## Key Assumptions

- FY2027 revenue and adjusted EPS are explicit consensus anchors. FY2028 headline revenue and adjusted EPS are public, but detailed later-year estimate tables are gated. **Every FY2029-FY2036 financial figure is extrapolated**, as are all scenario operating-income, GAAP-net-income, and FCF figures.
- Forward P/E is the price-setting method because net debt equals 5.69x TTM FCF. EV/EBITDA and FCF yield are cross-checks.
- Adjusted EPS excludes company-defined comparable items. GAAP net income includes impairment, divestiture, restructuring, investment, and tax items and therefore will not reconcile mechanically to adjusted EPS.
- Revenue growth in the year-5 and year-10 rows is the scenario annual rate, not growth since the previous displayed row.
- FCF yield uses projected free cash flow divided by projected market capitalization. The model assumes the share count continues declining, but more slowly as debt reduction receives greater priority.
- Bear/base/bull year-10 terminal forward P/E anchors are 8.5x, 12.5x, and 14.5x. These remain below the approximately 19x-20x forward multiples observed in FY2022-FY2024.
- The projections assume no transformative acquisition, no loss of the perpetual U.S. beer license, and no tariff regime severe enough to invalidate Mexican production economics.
- The $600 million September 2026 note redemption is treated as balance-sheet maintenance until Q2 filings confirm whether gross debt declined.
