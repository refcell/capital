## Read

- Bear is a profitable stagnation case, not a recessionary collapse. Its year-5 $17.60 target is below current price as required for a genuine downside scenario.
- Base tracks StockAnalysis's 4.2% three-year revenue-growth forecast and assumes modest operating leverage. The $30.10 year-5 value is 6% below the analyst average target.
- Bull requires North America to become a source of route-density leverage rather than recurring integration cost. Its year-5 value is above the current sell-side high and should not be treated as central.
- Price CAGRs exclude dividends. At the current 2.41% yield, dividends add cash return only if maintained.

## Key Assumptions

- FY2026 revenue and FY2027 headline revenue are the explicit public consensus anchors. **Every FY2028-FY2036 figure is extrapolated**, as are all scenario operating income, net income and FCF figures.
- Public adjusted EPS is provider-labelled non-GAAP. Because StockAnalysis mixes ordinary-share and ADR presentation, ADR EPS is independently anchored to ADR price divided by ADR forward P/E.
- FCF yield uses projected FCF divided by projected market capitalization. It is a cross-check, not the price-setting method.
- Revenue growth in year-5 and year-10 rows is the scenario annual rate, not growth since the previous displayed row.
- Net debt reduction is embedded indirectly through higher EPS and exit multiples; the table does not present an explicit debt schedule.
- The model assumes no transformative acquisition, no major goodwill impairment accompanied by cash deterioration, and approximately flat diluted ADR-equivalent shares.
- Bear/base/bull terminal P/E anchors are 10x, 14x and 16x. The range reflects route quality, leverage and evidence on Terminix integration.
