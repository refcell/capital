## Scenario Read

The one-year paths are intentionally narrower than the terminal outcomes. Public consensus already expects $3.69 of FY2026 EPS, and the average analyst target of $55.03 is near $54.90. Near-term upside therefore requires estimate revisions or clearer capital policy. The long-term range widens because modest differences in ROE, book-value compounding and terminal multiple accumulate.

The base case produces only 4.4% annual stock-price appreciation before dividends. That is consistent with the workbook's Watch stance and $55.62 present-value base case. The bull generates 10.6% annual appreciation, but it requires both operating success and regulatory permission to distribute capital. The bear loses 4.1% annually despite continuing profitability because the starting 1.88x book multiple compresses.

For UBS, the key terminal bridge is not FCF margin. It is normalized ROE relative to cost of equity. The TTM ROE of 10.67% is only modestly above the model's 8.94% CAPM estimate. A durable rise to 12.5%-15% supports the base and bull paths. A return toward 9% supports the bear, even if headline revenue remains stable.

## Key Assumptions

- FY2026 revenue of $53.68 billion, net income of $11.19 billion and EPS of $3.69 are anchored to the visible StockAnalysis consensus on September 3, 2026.
- FY2027, FY2028, FY2029, FY2031 and FY2036 are extrapolated because detailed public estimates beyond FY2026 were gated.
- All EPS figures are scenario diluted EPS and may differ from UBS' underlying, reported and currency-adjusted presentation.
- Free cash flow yield is N/A because conventional FCF is not meaningful for a deposit-funded bank.
- Bear diluted shares decline toward 2.90 billion by FY2036; base toward 2.70 billion; bull toward 2.50 billion. Each path assumes buybacks remain subordinate to CET1 and Swiss regulatory requirements.
- Bear normalized ROE trends toward 9%; base toward 12.5%; bull toward 15%. These are the central value drivers.
- Terminal P/E anchors are 9x bear, 12x base and 15x bull. The workbook separately uses terminal P/B multiples of 1.5x, 2.0x and 2.35x.
- Interim stock prices are calculated from constant annualized paths between $54.90 and the explicit FY2036 terminal prices. They are valuation paths, not analyst targets.
- Revenue growth is year over year for consecutive rows and annualized between non-consecutive displayed rows.
- The stock-price CAGR excludes dividends. UBS' capital return means total shareholder return could be higher, but the dividend path is sensitive to capital regulation.
