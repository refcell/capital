## Read

- Bear is a genuine downside case. Its US$13.84 year-5 value is 58% below the quote and close to the prior 52-week low, demonstrating the residual equity sensitivity to lower FCF and higher debt.
- Base follows the visible FY2027 revenue reset, then uses 2.5% revenue growth. The US$36 year-5 value is close to the US$37.01 analyst average target and matches the workbook.
- Bull requires structural improvement, not merely a higher oil-price deck. Its US$57.50 year-5 value is above all current sell-side targets and depends on operational and capital-allocation proof.
- Price paths work backward from explicit year-10 U.S.-dollar EPS/P-E anchors and pass through the workbook’s year-5 values. Price CAGRs exclude dividends.

## Key Assumptions

- FY2026 revenue and adjusted EPS and the FY2027 headline values are explicit public consensus anchors. Detailed FY2027-FY2028 tables are gated. **Every FY2028-FY2036 financial figure is extrapolated**, as are all scenario operating-income, net-income, FCF-yield, and U.S.-dollar EPS figures.
- Revenue, operating income, and net income are shown in Canadian dollars. Stock price and price targets are shown in U.S. dollars. Forward P/E uses modeled U.S.-dollar EPS rather than mixing currencies.
- The base case assumes the MEG acquisition contributes durable production and cost savings but does not annualize Q2’s inventory gain, working-capital release, or favorable commodity environment.
- Free-cash-flow yield uses projected U.S.-dollar FCF divided by projected U.S.-dollar market capitalization. It is a scenario cross-check, not a forecast supplied by analysts.
- The workbook’s primary method is normalized EV/FCF with explicit net-debt and share-count assumptions. The P/E paths here are a display mechanism required by the projection format and a cross-check on terminal values.
- Revenue growth in year-5 and year-10 rows is the scenario annual rate, not cumulative growth since the previous displayed row.
- Bear/base/bull year-10 terminal forward P/E anchors are 7.0x, 12.5x, and 15.0x. The range reflects the difference between commodity-cycle stress and proven integrated execution.
- The projection assumes no transformative acquisition after MEG, no permanent refinery closure, and no policy change that strands a material portion of the oil-sands reserve base.
- The October 30, 2026 earnings date is estimated. West White Rose timing, Christina Lake North ramp, refinery turnarounds, and February 2027 cash taxes can move the path materially.
