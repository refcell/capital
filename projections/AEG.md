## Notes and Extrapolation

1. **Revenue figures in $mm (USD)**: Original source data is in EUR (Yahoo Finance Financials tab). Conversion at ~1.0 EUR/USD for simplicity given the narrow FX band. Revenue is denominated primarily in EUR but reported in USD on Yahoo.
2. **EPS figures derived from net income / diluted shares**: Net income is estimated from pre-tax income minus a 6-7% effective tax rate (consistent with FY25 of €65M / €1,045M). Diluted shares decline over time from 1,898M (TTM) based on the observed buyback trajectory.
3. **P/E for insurance is distorted by GAAP**: Insurance P/Es are structurally elevated because GAAP net income excludes significant reserve mechanics, investment portfolio income, and actuarial adjustments. P/B is the cleaner valuation lens for insurance. The P/Es shown here are for mathematical consistency with the projection format, but the underlying valuation logic is P/B.
4. **FCF Yield is meaningful but modest**: Insurance FCF from operating cash flow (not capital-intensive), so the yield is real but low (4-6% range). This is not a growth FCF yield — it's a stable insurance cash flow yield.
5. **Buyback trajectory is the primary per-share driver**: Share count has declined 20% from FY23 to TTM (2,369M → 1,898M diluted). Continued buybacks at even 1/3 of the current pace drive significant per-share compounding in all scenarios.
6. **M&A uncertainty drives scenario dispersion**: The Rule 425 filings (May-Jun 2026) are the single most important variable. The bear case assumes dilutive M&A, the base case assumes neutral M&A, and the bull case assumes accretive M&A.
7. **10-year CAGR from current price**: Computed as `terminal_price / $9.46 ^ (1/10) - 1`. Bear: -31.7% / year → ~$6.50. Base: +5.7% / year → ~$10.00. Bull: +41.8% / year → ~$13.50.
8. **All FY2027+ figures are extrapolated** beyond the visible TTM and FY2025 data. No analyst estimates exist for AEG on Yahoo Finance analysis page. These are scenario-driven projections anchored in the workbook P/B framework.
9. **Terminal P/E anchors**: Bear 11.0x, Base 13.5x, Bull 16.0x. These are consistent with the workbook scenario P/B targets (0.75x, 1.00x, 1.45x) translated into P/E equivalents at the projected EPS levels.
