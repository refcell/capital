## Key Drivers

- **BVPS trajectory**: Current BVPS $94.17 at 1.15x P/B. If ROE sustains at 10-11% with 2.44% dividend payout, retained earnings compound BVPS at approximately 7-8% per year. The base case assumes 6% to account for continued integration and the acquired portfolio uncertainty.
- **P/B multiple**: At 1.15x, SSB is at the lower end of the peer range. Expansion toward 1.2-1.3x requires sustained ROE above 11% and clean credit metrics. Compression toward 0.85x is the bear case scenario.
- **Revenue growth**: Post-acquisition, the combined entity should see 3-5% annual revenue growth driven by NII expansion, deposit migration synergies, and modest loan growth. The TTM data shows essentially flat revenue ($2.66B) vs FY2025 ($2.62B) but improving net income, confirming the operating leverage thesis.
- **Share count**: Current 96.97M shares. Future dilution from acquisitions or equity raises is the primary per-share risk. No buyback program is currently announced.
