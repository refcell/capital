## Read

The bear case is a genuine failure case, not merely slower growth. It preserves a small residual value for cash, intellectual property, and non-VK2735 programs, but financing dilution and continued burn pull terminal NAV below today's pro-forma cash balance.

The base case produces only a 5.0% annualized stock-price return despite a commercial launch because today's price already capitalizes substantial clinical success. It is intentionally more conservative than the $94.61 average analyst target and close to the workbook's $52 base value. A modestly successful product can create company value while still producing only average per-share returns after dilution and commercialization costs.

The bull case requires broad franchise success and strategic execution, not just a positive trial. The injectable and oral formulations must both create value, maintenance must differentiate the label, and manufacturing must support a multibillion-dollar revenue base. The $180 terminal value is above current analyst targets because it represents a ten-year rather than 12-month horizon.

## Key Assumptions

- FY2027 and all later revenue rows are extrapolated beyond visible public revenue estimates; Viking currently has no product revenue consensus on the public StockAnalysis page.
- FY2026 adjusted EPS of negative $4.61 is the only detailed public annual estimate used. The FY2027 negative $4.40 headline is a directional loss anchor, not a complete consensus table.
- Prices are worked backward from explicit FY2036 terminal NAV anchors at a constant CAGR. This follows the projection-sheet convention while replacing terminal P/E with terminal per-share NAV because VKTX is a pre-revenue biotech.
- Revenue begins only after assumed regulatory and launch milestones. The first commercial years are intentionally stylized because trial outcomes, filing timing, approval timing, partnership structure, and net pricing are unknown.
- FCF yield is projected free cash flow divided by the projected equity value at each scenario's projected share price. Negative yields represent cash burn.
- P/E remains n/a while net income is negative. Positive P/E values use scenario net income divided by a scenario diluted share count that rises with financing: approximately 155M bear, 131M base, and 135M bull by FY2036.
- Bear assumes another major dilutive financing, unsuccessful broad VK2735 commercialization, and residual revenue from licensing or smaller assets.
- Base assumes one successful formulation, partnered launch economics, peak scenario revenue of $3.0B by FY2036, and moderate ongoing dilution.
- Bull assumes injectable and oral success, maintenance differentiation, adequate manufacturing, $8.0B FY2036 revenue, and better financing terms after de-risking.
- The model does not treat the September convertible coupon as the full economic cost of debt. The conversion option transfers upside to noteholders if the stock exceeds the initial approximately $50.75 conversion price.
