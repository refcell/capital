## Framework Notes

- Primary lens: Forward P/E — high-leverage FCF-insufficient framework. Net debt/FCF = 76x makes FCF multiples mathematically invalid.
- FCF Yield column omitted — would show near-zero or negative values that are meaningless for this company class.
- Revenue figures for Years 1-3 anchored in analyst consensus (Yahoo Finance analysis page, Aug 7, 2026). Years 5-10 extrapolated per scenario CAGR.
- Operating margin assumptions: Bear 6%, Base 8%, Bull 10%. These are calibrated against historical ranges (FY22: 7.1%, FY23: N/A due to loss, FY24: 8.1%, FY25: 6.3%, TTM: 6.3%).
- Tax rate: 19.0% TTM effective ($199M tax on $1,048M pretax income).
- Shares outstanding: Current 210.21M, assumed to continue declining modestly via buybacks (target ~205M by year 5 in base/bull, ~210M in bear if buybacks cease).
- D&A distortion: $1.054B TTM D&A (3.2% of revenue) suppresses GAAP net income. FFO (NI + D&A) is a better cash earnings proxy but not used for terminal P/E calibration.
- Interest expense trajectory: Bear $1.2B+, Base $1.1-1.2B, Bull $0.9-1.0B (declining as debt reduces).
