## Read

The projection emphasizes how little stock-price appreciation the base case offers from today's quote. A 10x terminal P/E on $18.32 of FY2036 EPS produces $183, only a 1.6% annual price CAGR. Dividends may lift total return, but a ten-year holding period should demand more than a high-single-digit total return for commodity and reserve risk.

The bear case illustrates equity sensitivity without assuming financial distress. Net debt is low, yet a lower earnings stream and lower multiple can still reduce the stock to the mid-$40s over ten years. Balance-sheet safety protects the enterprise from forced restructuring; it does not remove commodity beta from common shares.

The bull case requires both operations and allocation to work. Revenue reaches $8.5 billion, net margin reaches 18%, and the terminal P/E expands to 12x. Those assumptions are possible if Chord proves four-mile economics, sustains inventory, and shrinks shares. They are not public consensus and should not be mistaken for a forecast.

## Key Assumptions

- FY2026 revenue and adjusted EPS use the visible StockAnalysis / S&P Global consensus. FY2027 base revenue and EPS use the visible headline values. All FY2028-FY2036 rows are extrapolated because detailed public estimates were gated.
- The projection holds shares constant at 54.70 million. This is conservative if repurchases continue below intrinsic value and optimistic if acquisitions require material equity issuance.
- Operating and net margins are scenario assumptions, not management guidance. The bull case assumes operating leverage and capital efficiency; the bear case assumes commodity and inventory pressure.
- FCF yield uses scenario FCF margins of 9%-13% in bear, 18% in base, and 21%-22% in bull, divided by projected common market capitalization. It is not derived from projected net income.
- Revenue growth is YoY for consecutive annual rows. FY2031 growth is annualized from FY2029, and FY2036 growth is annualized from FY2031.
- Stock prices work backward from explicit year-10 terminal P/E anchors: 8x bear, 10x base, and 12x bull. Interim prices follow a constant CAGR from the current $157.00 price to each year-10 terminal value.
- P/E uses projected stock price divided by projected EPS, with EPS calculated from projected net income and 54.70 million shares.
- Dividends are excluded from stock-price CAGR. At the current $5.20 annualized dividend, total shareholder return would exceed the displayed price CAGR if the dividend is maintained.
- The workbook's probability-weighted five-year FCF value is $172.37. This projection uses a separate ten-year P/E method as a long-horizon cross-check, not a replacement for the workbook.
